# Cocaine, MDMA, amphetamine in Georgia — amounts and penalties

In a cocaine, ecstasy or amphetamine case, the amount decides almost everything — and the thresholds are tiny: 0.6 g of cocaine is already a 'large amount'. Below is the law's official table and the penalties. In such a case you need a lawyer before the first interview.

Obtaining or keeping a narcotic drug is punished under Article 260: above the small amount — up to 6 years in prison; a large amount — 5 to 8 years; especially large — 8 to 20 years or life. A small amount and use are an administrative offence — a GEL 500–2,000 fine or up to 60 days' detention. Ketamine and GHB are psychotropic substances, punished under Article 261.
New psychoactive substances — including what is sold as 'spice' or 'bio' — are treated separately: under Article 260 any amount is a crime, with up to 6 years in prison.

## At a club or party

- Handing drugs to a friend, even for free, is a transfer (Article 260²) — 10 to 15 years; selling — 12 to 20 years or life

- Searches and tests must follow the legal procedure — ask for a lawyer

- A person punished for drug use loses their driving licence for 3 years

- Anyone who voluntarily hands drugs over before an investigation starts, reporting it in writing or by phone, is released from criminal liability

## Questions & answers

### How many grams of cocaine is a crime?

Up to 0.06 g is a small amount — an administrative case. Above 0.06 g it's a criminal case, above 0.6 g a large amount (5 to 8 years), above 2 g especially large (8 to 20 years or life).

### How many ecstasy pills count as a large amount?

A large amount is 0.5 g of MDMA, and the MDMA content of a pill varies — the forensic examination establishes it. So even a few pills can count as a large amount.

### How much 'spice' is a crime?

Any amount of a new psychoactive substance is a crime — up to 6 years in prison.

### I'm a foreigner — what happens?

The rules are the same. You have the right to an interpreter and a lawyer; don't sign anything you don't understand. We work in English and Russian.
