# Smuggling and customs offences — lawyer in Georgia

We defend individuals and companies in cases of breaking the rules for moving goods across Georgia's customs border — from ordinary goods to jewellery and cultural heritage.

Moving a large amount of goods across the customs border past customs control, secretly, with a false document or with false data in the customs declaration is a crime (Article 214). A large amount means a customs value over GEL 15,000, an especially large amount over GEL 25,000; for goods hidden in a cache or brought by crossing the border illegally, the thresholds are GEL 5,000 and GEL 15,000.
So in these cases the key is often the correct customs valuation of the goods: below the threshold, the act is usually no longer a crime.

## Penalties under Article 214

- Large amount — a fine or 3 to 5 years in prison

- Especially large amount — a fine or 5 to 7 years

- Cultural heritage, precious-metal jewellery — a fine or 4 to 7 years

- Weapons, explosives or other dangerous substances — 5 to 8 years

- Repeatedly, by an official, with violence or as a group — 8 to 11 years; by an organised group — 11 to 15 years

## Questions & answers

### Is every customs violation a crime?

No. Criminal liability requires a large amount — a customs value over GEL 15,000 (over GEL 5,000 for goods in a cache). Smaller violations are usually dealt with in a different way.

### I forgot to declare gold jewellery — what happens?

Moving precious-metal jewellery in breach of the rules is punished under a separate part of Article 214. The circumstances matter a lot — contact a lawyer straight away.

### What is the penalty for smuggling?

For a large amount — a fine or 3 to 5 years; with aggravating circumstances — up to 15 years.
