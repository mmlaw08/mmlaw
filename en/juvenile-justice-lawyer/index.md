## What a juvenile justice lawyer does

A juvenile justice lawyer protects a child's rights in criminal proceedings — from questioning to court. The aim is for the child to go through the process with as little stress as possible and for the case to be decided with their future in mind.

## What parents should know

- A minor must have a lawyer during questioning and, as a rule, a parent or legal representative

- Specialised lawyers, prosecutors and judges work on juvenile cases

- In some cases diversion is possible — without criminal prosecution

- Juvenile cases are, as a rule, heard behind closed doors

## Mistakes to avoid

- Agreeing to the child being questioned without a lawyer

- Not using the possibility of diversion

- Sharing case details on social media

- Pressuring the child to "confess"

## Questions & answers

### From what age is a person criminally liable in Georgia?

From 14. Special rules of the Juvenile Justice Code apply to persons under 18.

### What is diversion?

Diversion allows a minor to avoid criminal prosecution by meeting certain conditions — for example mediation, community service or a rehabilitation programme. The decision is made by the prosecutor.

### Can any lawyer defend a minor?

No. Under the Code, lawyers, judges, prosecutors and investigators working on juvenile cases must be specialised.
