General questions

How do I choose a lawyer in Georgia?

Choose a lawyer with experience in your type of case, who gives you an honest assessment of the risks and agrees the fee in advance and in writing. At MMLAW the first consultation is free, so you can meet the lawyer before deciding.

How much does the initial consultation cost?

The initial consultation is free. We review your situation, explain your options and, if needed, propose terms for our engagement.

What documents should I bring?

Bring everything related to your case: ID, contracts, court decisions, notices, police or official reports and correspondence. If you don't have the documents, contact us anyway — we can help obtain them.

How long will my case take?

Every case is different. Some matters are settled through negotiation within weeks; court disputes can take months or longer. We give you a realistic estimate at the first consultation.

Do you work on a success-fee basis?

In certain categories of cases — yes. The fee structure and amount are agreed in advance and in writing, so there are no hidden costs.

Which languages do you work in?

We provide consultations and representation in Georgian, English and Russian — including for foreign nationals and international companies.

Where do you represent clients?

We represent clients before courts of all instances in Georgia — district (city) courts, courts of appeal and the Supreme Court — as well as before administrative bodies and investigative authorities.

What does a lawyer do?

A lawyer gives you legal advice, prepares documents and represents you in negotiations, before public authorities and in court. In criminal cases, a lawyer protects the rights of the accused from the first contact with the investigating authority.

How much does a lawyer cost in Georgia?

Fees depend on the complexity and scope of the case — they can be fixed, hourly or success-based. The first consultation is free, and the fee is agreed in advance and in writing.

Can I consult a lawyer online?

Yes. Consultations are available at our office, by phone, on WhatsApp or by video call — in English, Georgian and Russian.

Criminal defense

Open page

When do I have the right to a lawyer in Georgia?

From the moment of arrest or of being charged — and before any questioning. You have the right to remain silent and to insist on your lawyer being present.

How long can I be held without a court decision?

A person must be charged within 48 hours of arrest and brought before a court within the following 24 hours. If this doesn't happen, they must be released immediately.

What is a plea agreement?

An agreement between the prosecution and the accused on guilt and sentence, which must be approved by the court. A defense lawyer's participation is mandatory. Never sign anything without independent legal advice.

When should I contact a criminal defense lawyer?

As early as possible — ideally as soon as you are summoned for questioning, or during a search or arrest. Involving a lawyer early often decides the outcome: your lawyer helps you avoid mistakes when giving statements and protects your rights while evidence is gathered.

How much does a criminal defense lawyer cost?

It depends on the complexity, stage and scope of the case. The first consultation is free — we assess the case and agree the fee with you in advance and in writing.

Can I refuse to give a statement?

Yes. The accused has the right to remain silent — you can decline to answer questions and speak to a lawyer first.

Can a relative hire a lawyer for me?

Yes. When someone is detained, it is often family members who find the lawyer. Call us or message us on WhatsApp — we'll tell you what to do first.

Am I entitled to a free lawyer?

If you can't pay, or your case is one of mandatory defence (for example a minor or a jury trial) and you have no private lawyer, the state pays for your defence through the Legal Aid Service.

Drug offences

Open page

Is using drugs a crime in Georgia?

The first time, in a small amount — as a rule, no. It is an administrative offence: a fine of GEL 500–2,000 or administrative detention of up to 60 days. If it happens again after an administrative penalty, it becomes a criminal offence.

What is the penalty for buying or possessing drugs?

Under Article 260 of the Criminal Code — imprisonment of up to 6 years; for a large amount or other aggravating circumstances, 5 to 8 years; for an especially large amount or by an organised group, 8 to 20 years or life. Much depends on the amount and the evidence.

Is marijuana punished?

Buying or possessing a small amount of marijuana is an administrative offence — a GEL 500 fine. Using it in public places, in front of minors or near schools carries higher fines. Large amounts and sale are criminal offences.

Can I refuse a drug test?

Avoiding a test is itself an administrative offence — a fine of GEL 500–2,000 or administrative detention of up to 60 days. Call a lawyer before you decide.

If I hand drugs over voluntarily, am I liable?

By law, a person who voluntarily hands over the substance is released from criminal liability, unless the act contains another crime. It counts as voluntary only if declared before an investigation starts and the person actively helps with the seizure.

What other consequences can there be?

Besides a criminal record, a court may take away certain rights, including the right to drive, under the Law on Combating Drug Crime.

Cybercrime

Open page

What counts as cybercrime?

Unauthorised access to a computer system, unlawful use or damage of data, and using tools designed for such acts. Fraud, extortion and threats committed online are criminal offences too.

I was scammed online. Can I get my money back?

Sometimes, yes — especially if you act fast: contact your bank, keep all the evidence and talk to a lawyer. Don't trust services that promise to recover money for an upfront fee.

I'm being blackmailed with private photos. What should I do?

Don't pay and stop replying, but don't delete anything. Contact a lawyer right away — blackmail and sharing private images are criminal offences.

Can the police take my phone?

As a rule, seizing a device and examining its contents requires a court order. Talk to your lawyer before signing any record or giving out passwords.

Property crimes

Open page

What is the difference between theft, open theft and robbery?

Theft is secret taking; open theft (ძარცვა) is taking openly, in front of others; robbery is an attack with violence dangerous to life or health, or the threat of it. Penalties get stricter in that order.

What counts as significant damage and a large amount?

For property crimes, significant damage is a value over GEL 150, and a large amount is over GEL 10,000. Both make the penalty stricter.

I couldn't repay a debt. Is that fraud?

Not necessarily. Fraud requires an intent to deceive from the start. If an obligation simply wasn't met, it is often a civil dispute.

If I compensate the damage, will the case be dropped?

Compensation doesn't end the case automatically, but it can affect the sentence, the preventive measure or the terms of a plea agreement.

Economic crimes

Open page

Is any tax debt a crime?

No. Criminal liability arises for intentional tax evasion where the amount exceeds GEL 100,000. Most disputes are resolved with the tax authority and in court.

I received a tax demand. What should I do?

Contact a lawyer immediately. If within 45 working days of the demand the principal amount is paid, deferred or adjusted, there is no criminal liability.

What is money laundering?

Giving illegal or unexplained property a lawful appearance to hide its real origin. The penalty is 3 to 6 years, up to 12 with aggravating circumstances.

I'm a company director. Am I liable?

That depends on who made which decisions and what they knew. A lawyer checks whether responsibility has been assigned correctly.

Corruption and official crimes

Open page

I was asked for a bribe. What should I do?

Don't pay, and contact a lawyer immediately. A bribe-giver is released from liability if they report it voluntarily — this step must be taken correctly.

What is the penalty for taking a bribe?

6 to 9 years' imprisonment; for a large amount (over GEL 10,000) 7 to 11 years; for an especially large amount (over GEL 30,000), repeatedly or by extortion, 11 to 15 years.

Who counts as an official?

Public servants and persons treated as such, including employees of public-law legal entities who exercise public powers.

I was secretly recorded. Is that evidence?

Only if it was obtained in the way the law requires, with the necessary authorisation. Checking this is one of the main tasks of the defence.

Violent crimes

Open page

I was defending myself. Am I liable?

If it was lawful self-defence, the act is not unlawful. The question is whether the defence was clearly disproportionate to the attack. A lawyer proves this with evidence.

How is the severity of an injury decided?

By a medical examination. Minor, less serious and serious injuries fall under different articles — 1 to 3 years, 2 to 4 years and 4 to 7 years.

There was a fight and we were both hurt. What happens?

In such cases both sides can be both victim and accused. It is important that your version and evidence are recorded correctly from the start.

What is the penalty for homicide?

Intentional homicide carries 10 to 15 years; with aggravating circumstances, 16 to 20 years or life imprisonment.

Juvenile justice

Open page

Is my child entitled to a free lawyer?

Yes. An accused minor and a minor victim are entitled to free legal aid at any stage of a criminal case if they have no private lawyer. A minor witness is entitled if unable to pay or in certain cases defined by law.

From what age is a person criminally liable in Georgia?

From 14. Special rules of the Juvenile Justice Code apply to persons under 18.

What is diversion?

Diversion allows a minor to avoid criminal prosecution by meeting certain conditions — for example mediation, community service or a rehabilitation programme. The decision is made by the prosecutor.

Can any lawyer defend a minor?

No. Under the Code, lawyers, judges, prosecutors and investigators working on juvenile cases must be specialised.

Civil litigation

Open page

How are civil courts organised in Georgia?

First instance — district (city) courts; second instance — the Tbilisi and Kutaisi Courts of Appeal; the final, cassation instance — the Supreme Court of Georgia.

Can a dispute be resolved without going to court?

Yes — through negotiation, mediation or arbitration. For some categories of disputes (for example certain family, inheritance and neighbour disputes) court mediation may be mandatory.

How long do I have to file a claim?

It depends on the type of claim. Under the Civil Code of Georgia the general limitation period is 10 years, 3 years for contractual claims and 6 years for claims related to immovable property. Some claims have shorter periods, so it is best to act early.

Administrative law

Open page

What is the deadline to challenge an administrative act in court?

As a rule, one month from the date the act was officially delivered to you. Some cases have different statutory terms, so check the exact deadline immediately.

Do I have to file an administrative complaint first?

In some cases the law requires a complaint to the superior body before going to court; in others you may go straight to court. We assess which route is faster and stronger.

Can a fine be appealed?

Yes. A decision on an administrative offence can be appealed to the superior body or to the court within short statutory deadlines.

Labor disputes

Open page

What can I do if I was dismissed unlawfully?

You can ask your employer for the written grounds of dismissal and challenge it in court, seeking reinstatement or compensation. Deadlines are short (as a rule, 30 calendar days), so contact a lawyer immediately.

Is an oral employment contract valid?

An oral contract is possible, but a contract concluded for more than one month must be in writing.

Do you represent employees or employers?

Both. We help employees restore their rights, and help employers prevent disputes and represent them in court.

Corporate law

Open page

How long does it take to register a company in Georgia?

With properly prepared documents, registering an LLC with the National Agency of Public Registry usually takes one business day.

Can a foreigner own 100% of a Georgian company?

Yes. Foreign individuals and companies can be the sole founders of a Georgian LLC.

Which law governs companies in Georgia?

The Law of Georgia on Entrepreneurs (new version in force since 2022).

Real estate

Open page

Where is property ownership registered?

At the National Agency of Public Registry (NAPR). Ownership of real estate arises upon registration. A registry extract shows the owner and any encumbrances (mortgage, seizure).

Can foreigners buy property in Georgia?

Yes. Foreigners can freely buy apartments, houses and non-agricultural land. Purchase of agricultural land by foreigners is restricted by law.

What should I check before buying?

The registry extract (owner, mortgage, seizure, prohibitions), the seller's authority, permits for new construction, and any utility or other debts.

Family law

Open page

Can we divorce without going to court?

Yes — if both spouses agree and there is no dispute over children or property, the divorce can be registered with the Civil Registry (through the Public Service Hall). Otherwise the divorce is decided by a court.

How is property divided?

Property acquired during the marriage is the spouses' joint property and is, as a rule, divided equally unless a marriage contract says otherwise. Property owned before marriage, gifts and inheritance remain personal property.

How is alimony determined?

Parents must support their minor children. The amount depends on the child's needs and the parents' financial situation — set by agreement or by the court.

Inheritance

Open page

How long do I have to accept an inheritance?

6 months from the opening of the inheritance — as a rule, the date of death. You can accept it by applying to a notary or by actually taking possession of the property.

Who are the first-line heirs?

The deceased's children (including adopted children), spouse and parents. They inherit in equal shares unless a will provides otherwise.

What if I missed the 6-month deadline?

If you missed it for a valid reason, a court can extend it. Whether you actually took possession of the property also matters. We'll assess your case.

Can a will be challenged?

Yes — for example if the will's form was not respected or the testator was not aware of their actions when making it. Certain close relatives may also be entitled to a compulsory share regardless of the will.

Am I liable for the deceased's debts?

As a rule, an heir is liable for debts only up to the value of the property inherited. It's worth checking the debts before accepting — if needed, you can renounce the inheritance.

Is there inheritance tax in Georgia?

There is no separate inheritance tax. First- and second-order heirs — children, spouse, parents, siblings — pay no tax on property received by inheritance or gift. Third- and fourth-order heirs (grandparents, uncles, aunts) are exempt up to GEL 150,000 a year. Otherwise the value is taxed as income at 20%.

Intellectual property

Open page

Where are trademarks registered in Georgia?

With Sakpatenti — the National Intellectual Property Center of Georgia. International registration is possible through the Madrid system.

How long does trademark protection last?

10 years from the filing date, renewable every 10 years.

Does copyright need to be registered?

No — copyright arises automatically when a work is created. Depositing the work with Sakpatenti helps prove authorship.

Fraud cases

Open page

Is failing to repay a debt fraud?

Not necessarily. Fraud requires intent to deceive from the start. If an obligation simply wasn't met, it is often a civil dispute.

I was scammed — can I get my money back?

The earlier you act, the better the chances: a police report, freezing the suspect's property and the compensation mechanism. In large-scale cases the convicted person is ordered to pay compensation.

What is the penalty for fraud?

For the basic offence — from a fine up to 4 years in prison; with aggravating circumstances — up to 10 years.

Financial crime victims

Open page

Which crimes does compensation cover?

For example: large-scale fraud, extortion and embezzlement, property damage over GEL 10,000, abuse of authority in a company and large-scale computer crime.

What if the convicted person leaves the country?

If they are a Georgian citizen or a person with status in Georgia, they are banned from leaving until compensation is paid — unless you waive it in writing.

Can I also file a civil claim?

Yes. Compensation doesn't take away your right to a civil claim — you just can't claim again the part you already received.

Tax disputes

Open page

How long do I have to appeal a tax decision?

30 days from receiving it. Complaints are usually filed electronically.

Can tax debt be reduced?

Yes, in cases set by law a tax settlement can be signed with the Revenue Service to reduce part of the debt.

Money laundering cases

Open page

My bank suspended a transaction — what should I do?

Ask for a written explanation and prepare documents proving where the money came from. If it reaches an investigation, involve a lawyer straight away.

What is unexplained property?

Property for which the person or their relatives have no documents proving it was lawfully obtained.

What is the penalty for money laundering?

For the basic offence — a fine or 3 to 6 years; on an especially large scale or by an organised group — up to 12 years.

Is 'renting out' my account or card risky?

Yes. If someone else's money comes into your account and you transfer or withdraw it, you may end up accused as a participant in fraud or of money laundering — even if you only took a 'small fee'.

Asset freezing and confiscation

Open page

My account has been frozen — what should I do?

Find out in which case and on what grounds. The court order can be appealed — contact a lawyer straight away.

Can a family member's property be frozen?

It can, if they are treated as a person connected to the accused. Then it is important to prove the property was obtained lawfully and independently.

What is the difference between a freeze and confiscation?

A freeze is temporary — it restricts dealing with the property. Confiscation is final: the property passes to the state.

Abuse-of-office cases

Open page

Can a private company director be liable?

Not under Article 332, but possibly under Article 220 — if they used their authority against the company's interests for gain and caused significant damage.

Is every mistake at work a crime?

No. There must be a substantial breach of rights or interests, and for abuse of authority also an aim of gain or advantage.

Smuggling and customs cases

Open page

Is every customs violation a crime?

No. Criminal liability requires a large amount — a customs value over GEL 15,000 (over GEL 5,000 for goods in a cache). Smaller violations are usually dealt with in a different way.

I forgot to declare gold jewellery — what happens?

Moving precious-metal jewellery in breach of the rules is punished under a separate part of Article 214. The circumstances matter a lot — contact a lawyer straight away.

What is the penalty for smuggling?

For a large amount — a fine or 3 to 5 years; with aggravating circumstances — up to 15 years.

Director liability and insolvency

Open page

Is a director personally liable for an LLC's debts?

Generally no — the company is liable with its own assets. But a director can be liable if they breached their duties and caused damage, or if a crime is established.

The company can't pay its debts — what should I do?

Assess the situation with a lawyer as early as possible: rehabilitation or an agreement with creditors may be better than liquidation.

I'm a creditor — how do I protect my claim?

Register the claim in the insolvency case on time and, if needed, ask the court for interim measures.

Drug possession cases

Open page

How many grams of marijuana is a crime in Georgia?

Up to and including 5 grams of dried marijuana is a small amount — a GEL 500 administrative fine. More than 5 grams is already a criminal case under Article 260; over 70 grams is a large amount, over 250 grams especially large.

What is the penalty under Article 260, Part 1?

Up to 6 years in prison. Part 2 — 5 to 8 years; Part 3 — 8 to 20 years or life.

Is the quantity counted with packaging or fillers?

No. By law the quantity is the pure substance, without fillers. That is why checking the lab report matters so much.

Removing a criminal record

Open page

How do I get a criminal record certificate?

The certificate is issued by the MIA Service Agency — online on its website or app, or at a branch; GEL 20, 3 working days. For use abroad it usually needs an apostille. If the certificate still shows a conviction, we check whether it has expired and whether it can be removed early.

When will my criminal record expire?

It depends on the sentence and the category of crime: for a suspended sentence — after the probation period; for imprisonment — 3, 6 or 8 years after serving it.

Can a criminal record be removed early?

Yes. If you have behaved impeccably since serving the sentence, a court can remove the record at your request before the period ends.

What does a removed record mean?

An expired or removed record is no longer taken into account — for example, when a new case is classified or a sentence is set.

Early release on parole

Open page

When is early release possible?

For a less serious crime — after half of the sentence, for a serious crime — two-thirds, for an especially serious crime — three-quarters. In every case at least 6 months must be served.

Who decides?

For imprisonment — the local board of the Special Penitentiary Service; for other sentences — a court.

Can the family hire a lawyer?

Yes. A family member can contact us; we meet the prisoner and prepare the case.

Pyramid scheme and investment fraud cases

Open page

I lost money in a pyramid scheme — can I get it back?

The earlier you act, the better the chances: a report, a freeze on property and the compensation mechanism. In large-scale fraud the convicted person is ordered to pay compensation.

I brought friends into the scheme — am I liable?

If you didn't know the scheme was a deception, that is the core of your defence. Talk to a lawyer before giving a statement to investigators.

The platform is abroad — what can be done?

A case can be opened in Georgia if the victim or the recipient of the money is here; international cooperation is used where needed. Contacting your bank quickly matters too.

Help with blackmail over private photos

Open page

If I pay, will they stop?

Often not. Paying shows the pressure works, and demands grow. It is better to keep the evidence and contact a lawyer and the police.

The photo has already been posted — can it be removed?

Yes. We use platforms' takedown tools and, where needed, legal routes — while the criminal case continues.

Will my name appear anywhere?

We work in complete confidence. There are also ways to protect a victim's private life during the investigation and in court — your lawyer will ask for them.

Time-barred loan cases

Open page

How long until a loan is time-barred?

The limitation period for a loan claim is 3 years, 6 years for contracts concerning real estate. If a court has already confirmed the debt — 10 years.

Is a time-barred loan cancelled automatically?

No. After the period expires the debtor may refuse to pay, but must raise it in court themselves. The court does not apply the time bar on its own initiative.

A collector asked me to pay a small amount — what should I do?

Check the period before you pay. Paying part of the debt or interest, or acknowledging it, restarts the limitation period.

Does a time-barred loan stay in my credit history?

According to the National Bank's financial education portal, a time-barred debt stays in the credit history for 5 years, after which you can ask for it to be removed.

Restraining and protective order cases

Open page

How long does a restraining order last?

Up to 1 month. A protective order is issued by a court for up to 9 months and can be extended by 3 more.

Can a restraining order be appealed?

Yes. Both the victim and the person it was issued against can appeal within 3 days of receiving it. The deadline is very short — contact a lawyer immediately.

What happens if an order is breached?

Breaching an order is a crime: a fine, community service or up to 1 year in prison; repeatedly — 1 to 3 years.

Is the order cancelled after reconciliation?

Not automatically. It can be cancelled on a joint application if the victim or other family members are no longer at risk.

Removing a seizure on accounts and property

Open page

My account was seized — how do I find out why?

A seizure is imposed within an enforcement case. We can check the case, the creditor and the amount, and tell you the fastest way to lift it.

The tax office put an „inkaso“ on my account — what does it mean?

It is a collection order: the Revenue Service takes the amount of a confirmed tax debt from your account. If you disagree with the debt, you can appeal — while the dispute lasts, the obligation to pay is suspended.

Can my salary be seized too?

By law money can be collected from salary and other income, but income below the subsistence minimum and targeted state assistance are protected from seizure.

A seizure from an old case is still active — what can I do?

If 10 years have passed since enforcement proceedings for a money claim began, enforcement must end and the seizure be lifted — except in cases set by law, such as alimony.

How long do I have to appeal an enforcement officer's action?

15 calendar days from the action — to the head of the National Bureau of Enforcement.

Pardon and amnesty

Open page

What is the difference between a pardon and an amnesty?

A pardon is granted by the President to a specific person on request. An amnesty is declared by Parliament through a law and applies to everyone who meets its conditions.

Who can request a pardon?

The convicted person or a family member. The Pardon Commission reviews the request and the President decides.

Is there an amnesty in 2026?

An amnesty can only be declared by a law passed by Parliament. If such a law is adopted, we will check whether it covers your article and sentence.

Driving licence suspension cases

Open page

How long will I lose my licence for drink-driving?

At 0.3–0.7‰ — 6 months; over 0.7‰ or refusing the test — 1 year. With minor damage — 3 years and a GEL 2,000 fine.

Will my licence be suspended for an unpaid fine?

Yes. If a traffic fine isn't paid within 30 days, and then within another 30 days with a surcharge, the licence is suspended.

Can I appeal?

Yes. The decision can be appealed within 10 days of receiving it. A lawyer checks the test procedure, the record and the video.

Do I need to retake the exam afterwards?

After a suspension for an unpaid fine, the licence is restored when the term ends and no exam is required. In other cases the rule needs to be checked.

Appealing fines

Open page

How long do I have to appeal a fine?

10 days from receiving the decision. If you missed it for a valid reason, you can ask for the deadline to be restored.

Can a parking or bus-lane fine be appealed too?

Yes. These are administrative offences too — for example, breaking parking rules in Tbilisi falls under Article 125² — and the decision can be appealed within 10 days of receipt.

Do I have to pay while appealing?

An appeal pauses the payment deadline until it is decided. If it is rejected, the fine must be paid.

Someone else was driving my car — what should I do?

That is a common ground for appeal. Say who was driving and provide evidence.

Compensation for moral damage

Open page

When can I claim moral damage?

Only in cases set by law — for example, injury, culpable harm to honour, dignity or private life, or unlawful detention or conviction.

How much will a court award?

There is no fixed rate. The court sets a 'reasonable and fair' amount based on the severity of the harm, fault and practice.

I was unlawfully detained — am I owed compensation?

A rehabilitated person is compensated by the state for damage from unlawful conviction or detention — regardless of officials' fault.

Establishing paternity

Open page

How is paternity established in court?

Mainly by DNA testing. If that's impossible, the court considers living together, raising or supporting the child together, and other evidence of acknowledgment.

Who can apply to establish paternity?

A parent, the child's guardian, the person supporting the child, or the child after reaching adulthood.

Can child support be claimed too?

Yes. Once paternity is established, the child has the same rights as a child of married parents — including the right to support.

Taking a child abroad — consent and disputes

Open page

Can I take my child abroad without the other parent's consent?

By law, for temporary travel of a child under 16, one parent's permission and an adult escort are enough. But a passport usually needs both parents' consent, and in a dispute a court decides.

The other parent won't consent to a passport — what can I do?

In some cases one parent's consent is enough (e.g. the other parent's whereabouts are unknown or travel is needed for treatment). Otherwise you can go to court.

The other parent took our child abroad and won't return them — what should I do?

If the child is in a Hague Convention country, you can ask for the child's return. Act quickly — time matters a lot in these cases.

Challenging a dismissal

Open page

How long do I have to challenge a dismissal?

The deadlines are very short: ask for written reasons within 30 days, then sue within 30 days of receiving them. If you didn't ask for reasons, you have 30 days from receiving the dismissal notice.

Can a pregnant woman be dismissed?

Dismissal is prohibited from notifying the employer of pregnancy until the end of maternity and childcare leave — except in cases listed by law, such as the end of a fixed term, resignation or a serious breach of duties.

What compensation do I get for redundancy?

With 30 days' written notice — at least 1 month's salary; with 3 days' notice — at least 2 months' salary. Pay for unused leave comes on top.

Can I be dismissed during probation?

Yes. A probation period (up to 6 months) can be ended at any time and the Article 48 rules don't apply unless the contract says otherwise. The time worked must still be paid.

Protecting employee rights — leave, maternity, salary

Open page

How many days of leave am I entitled to?

At least 24 working days of paid leave and at least 15 calendar days of unpaid leave a year. A contract can give more, never less.

How long is maternity leave?

126 days for pregnancy and childbirth (143 with complications or twins), then childcare leave. In total 183 days (or 200) are paid, by the state.

My salary is late — what can I do?

For every day of delay the employer owes you 0.07% of the amount. Send a written demand; if there's no agreement within 14 days, you can go to court.

Is overtime paid?

Yes — at an increased hourly rate agreed by the parties, or with extra time off by agreement. Pregnant women, breastfeeding mothers, parents of children under 3 and minors can't be made to work overtime without their consent.

Removal from the debtors' register

Open page

How do I find out if I'm on the debtors' register?

The register's data are public. The National Bureau of Enforcement issues an extract no later than the next working day after the application, for a fee. We can check which case and which creditor you're registered for.

I paid the debt — will I be removed automatically?

Paying in full is a ground for removal, and all restrictions are lifted on removal. If the entry hasn't been removed, you need to contact the enforcement officer — we can help.

I'm on the register — can I sell my flat?

Only with consent: in cases for the state budget — the National Bureau of Enforcement's; in other cases — the Bureau's and the creditor's.

I'm on the register for a very old debt — what can I do?

If 10 years have passed since enforcement of a money claim began, enforcement must end and you must be removed — except in cases set by law, such as alimony.

Marital property division cases

Open page

The flat is in my spouse's name — is it still joint?

If it was bought during the marriage, yes — whoever it's registered to, unless a marriage contract says otherwise.

Is a flat bought before marriage divided?

Usually not — it's personal property. But if its value grew significantly through joint spending during the marriage (renovation, completing construction), a court may treat it as joint.

How long do I have to ask for division?

After divorce the limitation period is 3 years. When it starts running can be disputed, so don't delay.

Who pays the mortgage after division?

Joint debts are split between the spouses in proportion to their shares of joint property. With the bank, though, the loan contract still applies — so this needs to be settled separately in the agreement or lawsuit.

Making and challenging a will

Open page

Is a handwritten will valid?

Yes — a handwritten will is valid if the testator wrote it by hand and signed it. But it is easier to contest, so we recommend a notarial will.

Can I leave my child nothing at all?

Children, parents and the spouse are entitled to a compulsory share regardless of the will — half of their share under the law. The exception is when an heir has lost the right to inherit under the law, for example as an unworthy heir.

How long do I have to challenge a will?

2 years from the inheritance opening. After that, the will can no longer be challenged.

There are several wills — which one applies?

A newer will revokes the older one to the extent they conflict. A notarial will, however, takes priority and cannot be revoked by a will in another form.

Debt recovery

Open page

I lent money without an IOU — can I get it back?

Possibly. Evidence can be a bank transfer, messages, an audio recording or a witness. The sooner you contact us, the more evidence we can preserve.

How long do I have to claim the debt?

The limitation period is 3 years — usually from the repayment date. After that the debtor can rely on the limitation period and the court will reject the claim.

How much does a lawsuit cost?

The court fee is 3% of the amount claimed, but at least 100 GEL. If you win, the losing party usually reimburses the court costs.

The debtor has no assets — what happens?

The judgment goes to enforcement and the debtor is entered into the debtors register: they can't register new property or a car without the enforcement bureau finding out. Money can also be deducted from their salary.

Contract drafting and review

Open page

Is a verbal agreement a contract?

Often — yes, if the law doesn't require a particular form. But a verbal agreement is hard to prove. Real estate, however, can only be acquired with a written contract and registration.

Do I need to have a contract certified by a notary?

Not always. Notarisation is required by law in some cases and useful in others: for example, a debt under a notarised loan agreement can be recovered even without going to court.

How high can a contractual penalty be?

The parties set it freely, but the agreement must be in writing. If the penalty is excessive, a court can reduce it; for loans, the law sets a cap.

Is a contract in English valid?

Yes, a contract can be made in any language. You may need a Georgian translation for the Public Registry or a court, so we often prepare a bilingual text.

Gambling ban for a family member

Open page

Who can apply to the court?

A parent, spouse, child or someone who lives permanently with the person.

How much does it cost and how long does it take?

The application is free of court fees. The judge issues an order within 3–5 working days and it takes effect immediately.

Does the ban cover online casinos?

Yes. It covers casinos, slot halls, betting and online gambling — when the operator holds a Georgian permit.

Can a person ban themselves?

Yes — by applying to the Revenue Service. They are also listed for 5 years.

Who is barred from gambling by law?

Georgian citizens under 25, foreigners under 18, people employed in public institutions, the National Bank and regulators, and family members registered in the database of socially vulnerable families.

Gambling debts and family property

Open page

Can my spouse's gambling debt be collected from our flat?

Only from their share. Joint property can be used only if a court finds the money served the family's common interests. Money lost gambling usually doesn't.

Is a loan taken for gambling void?

No — the purpose of a loan doesn't affect its validity. But interest and penalties above the legal limits can be reduced, and you can refuse to pay a time-barred debt.

Am I liable for my child's debt?

No, unless you are a guarantor or co-borrower. An adult child's debt is collected only from their own property.

How do I stop new debts?

A family member applies to the court to place the person on the dependent list — after that they cannot gamble at any licensed casino or betting operator for 5 years.

Apartment for a lump sum (girao)

Open page

Is girao legal?

Yes — the law expressly allows a home to secure a loan when it is handed to the lender to live in. The key is a notarised contract and a mortgage registered in the registry.

What if the owner doesn't give my money back?

If the mortgage is registered, you recover the money from the flat through enforcement. Without registration you are an ordinary creditor, in line with everyone else.

Is a notary required?

Yes. A mortgage securing a loan must be notarised, and it takes effect only when registered in the Public Registry.

The flat already has a bank mortgage — can I still do girao?

You can, but the bank has priority and gets paid first. We advise against it, or recommend extra protection terms.

Apartment lease agreement

Open page

Does a lease need a notary?

No. But to register the lease, signatures must be certified or both parties must sign at the registry.

The flat was sold — do I have to move out?

No. If the flat was already handed to you, the new owner takes over the lease.

What tax does the landlord pay?

An individual renting out housing for living purposes without claiming expenses pays 5% of the rent.

Can the lease be in English or Russian?

Yes. We usually prepare a bilingual text — Georgian plus English or Russian — for the registry and courts.

Mortgage

Open page

Can I borrow from a private person against my flat?

As an individual, generally not — only a supervised or registered lender can make loans secured by real estate. Girao is the exception.

Can the flat pass to the creditor automatically?

No. Ownership passes only on a joint application by owner and creditor; otherwise the money is recovered through enforcement.

My loan has very high interest — what can I do?

The effective annual rate must not exceed 50%, and the law caps penalties and charges. You can refuse to pay the excess — we'll check the contract.

Land registration

Open page

Is systematic registration still free?

No. The systematic registration reform ended on 1 January 2026. Land is now registered on your own application, by the sporadic procedure.

I have no documents — can I register my land?

Possibly. The law accepts many old documents — from household books to land-tax lists. For self-occupied land, witness testimony can also count.

How much does land registration cost?

Agricultural land — GEL 50 (300 in Tbilisi and Batumi), non-agricultural — GEL 300, plus surveying. Self-occupied land — GEL 600.

My neighbour's plot overlaps mine — what now?

First notary mediation, within 3 weeks. If you can't agree, a court decides. We prepare the boundary evidence and represent you.

Building on agricultural land

Open page

Can I build a house on agricultural land?

On a private plot — often yes, if it fits the zone or surrounding buildings. Outside settlements, no permit is issued for housing. We'll check your plot.

Who changes land designation?

The Public Registry — where there is a justified need, based on a commission's opinion.

What does a change of designation cost?

The registration fee is GEL 50. Compensation applies only in Tbilisi and Batumi (GEL 34,001/ha) and special-regulation territories (GEL 100,000/ha).

Bringing medication into Georgia

Open page

Can I bring Lyrica (pregabalin) into Georgia?

Yes, for treatment — with medical documents and a customs declaration. Without documents a criminal case can start, because pregabalin is controlled in Georgia.

Customs took my medicine — what should I do?

Don't give a statement without a lawyer and keep all documents. We check which article applies and work to close the case if the medicine was for your treatment.

I'm only in transit — does this apply to me?

Yes. The law covers people in transit too: carry the medicine with medical and customs documents.

Lyrica, tramadol and prescription pills

Open page

How much Lyrica is a crime?

More than 0.6 g of pregabalin is already a criminal case; a large amount is over 6 g. The medicine form counts, so a few capsules can be enough.

I have a prescription — is it still a crime?

Medicine prescribed by a doctor and lawfully obtained is not a crime. Keep the prescription and proof of purchase.

What is the penalty for tramadol?

Basic — a fine or corrective labour up to 1 year; in a large amount — 3 to 7 years; especially large — 7 to 12 years.

Drug dealing charges

Open page

I gave some to a friend for free — is that dealing?

Free transfer is a separate crime (Article 260²) — 3 to 8 years for marijuana, 10 to 15 years for other narcotic drugs.

How is keeping different from dealing?

Keeping falls under Article 260 and is punished less severely; for dealing, the prosecution must prove a sale or transfer. That is often the key issue for the defence.

What rights does a convicted person lose?

A dealer loses the listed rights for 15 years, and medical and pharmaceutical work for 20 years. A plea agreement can reduce this.

Drug tests

Open page

The positive result is wrong — what can I do?

Within 10 days ask for an alternative test of the sealed control sample, and contact us — the result can also be challenged in court.

Will I lose my driving licence?

A court takes away the driving licence of a person punished for drug use for 3 years.

Cocaine, ecstasy and other club drugs

Open page

How many grams of cocaine is a crime?

Up to 0.06 g is a small amount — an administrative case. Above 0.06 g it's a criminal case, above 0.6 g a large amount (5 to 8 years), above 2 g especially large (8 to 20 years or life).

How many ecstasy pills count as a large amount?

A large amount is 0.5 g of MDMA, and the MDMA content of a pill varies — the forensic examination establishes it. So even a few pills can count as a large amount.

How much 'spice' is a crime?

Any amount of a new psychoactive substance is a crime — up to 6 years in prison.

I'm a foreigner — what happens?

The rules are the same. You have the right to an interpreter and a lawyer; don't sign anything you don't understand. We work in English and Russian.

Tax on freelance and foreign income in Georgia

Open page

Do I pay tax on money from Upwork or Fiverr?

Yes, if you do the work in Georgia. As a sole trader with small business status — 1%; unregistered — 20% through an annual return.

How do I register as a sole trader?

At the Public Service Hall, with your ID and an address. Then you apply for small business status to the Revenue Service on rs.ge.

What is the 1% limit?

GEL 500,000 a year. Above it, all income is taxed at 3% from that month to the end of the year; if you exceed it 2 years in a row, the status is cancelled.

Can a foreign salary be taxed at 1%?

No. Salary is excluded from the 1%. If you genuinely provide services rather than work as an employee, the contract must show it — we review the contract.

What if I don't file the annual return?

The penalty is 5% of the tax, 10% if more than 2 months late, plus interest of 0.05% per day.

Do we withhold tax for a remote employee living abroad?

For services performed abroad, as a rule, no; for management and financial services — 10%. Salary depends on the details — contact us before signing the contract.

Criminal record certificate

Open page

How do I get a criminal record certificate online?

On the MIA Service Agency website or app, after registering. It costs GEL 20 and takes 3 working days.

How long is the certificate valid?

15 calendar days.

Can someone else get it for me?

Yes — with your written consent or a power of attorney.

The certificate shows an old conviction — what can I do?

We check whether it has expired. If not, it may be possible to have it removed early through the court.

For foreigners

Open page

Can I work with you remotely?

Yes. Most matters are handled online and by phone; where a signature is needed in Georgia, we can act for you under a notarised power of attorney.

Do you work in English and Russian?

Yes. Consultations, documents and correspondence are available in Georgian, English and Russian.

Do my foreign documents need an apostille?

It depends on the country: documents from some countries need an apostille, others need consular legalisation, and documents from some countries need no extra certification at all. A certified Georgian translation is usually required. We'll tell you what your documents need.

I'm a foreigner and was detained in Georgia. What are my rights?

You have the right to remain silent, to a lawyer before any questioning, to a free interpreter and to have your consulate informed. Ask for a lawyer immediately and don't sign anything you don't understand.

Will my consulate be informed?

Yes, if you ask. A detained foreigner has the right to request that the consulate of their country be informed of the detention.

Can my family hire a lawyer from abroad?

Yes. A family member can contact us by phone or WhatsApp; we sign the engagement remotely and step into the case immediately.

How many days do I have to appeal a deportation decision?

10 days from when it is served. Contact a lawyer right away.

Can you handle my case if I'm already abroad?

Yes. We act under a power of attorney and keep you informed at every step.

Does paying the overstay fine cancel the entry ban?

No. For an overstay, the fine and the entry ban are imposed together.

Does buying property give me a residence permit?

Owning property can be a basis for a short-term residence permit if its value meets the threshold set by law (since 1 March 2026 — more than USD 150,000). The permit is not automatic — you have to apply for it.

Can I buy property without coming to Georgia?

Yes. You can give a notarised power of attorney (with an apostille if the country requires it) to your lawyer or a trusted person, who signs and registers the purchase for you.

Who pays tax when an apartment is bought?

The buyer pays the Public Registry registration fee. An individual seller who has owned the flat for more than 2 years pays no income tax on the gain from the sale.

Can I buy a flat in Georgia without residence or citizenship?

Yes. A foreigner can buy a flat, house or non-agricultural land without a residence permit or Georgian citizenship. Only agricultural land is restricted.

The developer is late — what can I do?

Depending on the contract, you can claim the penalty, damages, or terminate the contract and get your money back. We start by reviewing the contract.

Can you handle this while I live abroad?

Yes. We act for you under a power of attorney — in negotiations and in court.

What should I do before paying for a flat?

Check the developer, the landowner, mortgages and permits, and have a lawyer read the contract. It is the cheapest protection there is.

How much does company registration cost?

1 800 ₾ if you are in Georgia and 2 700 ₾ if registration is done remotely. The price is final and includes VAT; state fees are paid separately.

Do you guarantee the bank will open an account?

No. The bank decides and can refuse even without giving a reason. We prepare the documents and help make your application as complete as possible.

Can a foreigner own 100% of a company in Georgia?

Yes. Foreign individuals and companies can be the sole founder and director of a Georgian LLC.

Do I need to be in Georgia to register a company?

Not necessarily. Registration can be done through a representative with a notarised power of attorney. Opening a bank account usually needs more preparation, as each bank runs its own checks.

How are companies taxed in Georgia?

Profit tax (15%) is generally paid only when profit is distributed, for example as dividends. Small-business status for individual entrepreneurs offers a 1% turnover tax up to an annual limit. We check which regime fits your activity.

Do individual entrepreneurs need the right to work?

Yes. A self-employed foreigner, including an individual entrepreneur, needs it to work in Georgia.

Who is not covered by the rule?

For example, foreigners with a permanent residence permit. We check other exemptions for your case.

What happens if annual income exceeds GEL 500,000?

From the start of that month until the end of the year, all income is taxed at 3%.

How long does a residence permit application take?

Standard — 30 days (300 GEL). Expedited — 20 days (450 GEL) or 10 days (600 GEL). An application by a Georgian citizen's spouse — up to 90 days.

Can a refusal be appealed?

Yes — in court, within 10 calendar days of receiving the decision. The deadline is very short, so contact us as soon as you are refused.

Do I need a work permit to work in Georgia?

Since 1 March 2026, foreigners need the right to labour activity to work or do business in Georgia — except holders of a permanent residence permit and other cases set by law. We'll check what applies to you before you start work.

How long can I stay without a visa?

Citizens of listed countries — a full year; others — 90 days in any 180. The old 'Remotely from Georgia' digital-nomad programme is not currently active — for a longer stay you need a residence permit.

Can foreigners divorce in Georgia?

Often yes — especially if the marriage was registered in Georgia or one spouse lives here. Which court is competent and which country's law applies depends on the case, so get advice first.

Is a foreign court decision valid in Georgia?

Before it can be enforced, a foreign court decision generally has to be recognised in Georgia. Recognition is handled by the Supreme Court of Georgia; we prepare the application and the translated documents.

What if the other parent took our child abroad?

If a child has been wrongfully removed to or kept in a country that is party to the Hague Convention, you can ask for the child's return. Act quickly — time is critical in these cases.

Which is better — a 1% sole trader or the virtual zone?

For a freelancer working alone, the 1% sole trader is the simplest. The virtual zone is for companies and makes sense when you mainly supply IT abroad and plan to distribute profit. The right choice depends on income, clients and plans.

Can a newly formed company get international company status?

Yes, if the 2 years of IT experience belong to the foreign company it represents, or to shareholder companies holding over 50%. The work must still really be done in Georgia.

How long does virtual zone status take?

No later than 10 working days from the application, and the certificate within 2 working days of the status being granted.

Is registering as a sole trader enough to work in Georgia?

Not since 1 March 2026. You also need the right to work and a suitable residence permit or visa.

Can a foreign company get a permit?

Not directly. Permits are issued only to businesses registered in Georgia, so a Georgian company has to be registered first — we can handle that.

What is an 'international' online permit?

It is an online casino, slots or betting permit under which Georgian citizens may not be admitted to play.

How many websites does one online permit cover?

For online casino and slot games, one permit covers only one website on one domain name.

How many years must I live in Georgia to get citizenship?

By the ordinary route — the last 10 years, lawfully and continuously. The spouse of a Georgian citizen — 5 years. A stateless person with status — 5 years.

Can I keep my own citizenship?

Only with an exceptional grant. Through the ordinary route or marriage you must give up your previous citizenship.

My father is Georgian — am I entitled to citizenship?

If a parent was a Georgian citizen when you were born, you are a citizen by birth. For adults, Georgian roots count only for an exceptional grant.

I was born in Georgia in the Soviet era — what can I get?

Special rules apply to people born in Georgia, but only until 1 January 2027. The conditions depend on your date of birth, your situation in 1993 and whether you hold another citizenship — contact us in good time.

How long does the decision take?

By law — 3 months. The President makes the decision.

Can two foreigners marry in Georgia?

Yes, if both are in Georgia lawfully. There is no citizenship requirement.

How quickly can we get married?

There is no mandatory waiting period. The marriage is registered at the time stated in the application — often the same day.

Will our marriage be recognised in our country?

Many countries recognise a marriage concluded abroad under local law. The certificate needs an apostille and a translation. The exact recognition rules depend on your country's law — we check them in advance.

Is a church wedding enough?

No. Only a marriage registered by the state has legal effect.

Is a marriage in Georgia recognised in Russia?

Yes. Under Article 158 of the RF Family Code, a marriage concluded abroad under local law is valid in Russia without re-registration. The certificate needs an apostille and a notarised Russian translation.

Can I drive in Georgia on my own country's licence?

Yes — until it expires, but for no more than 1 year from your last entry into Georgia. A licence not in Latin or Georgian letters needs a certified translation.

Do I have to take an exam to exchange my licence?

No. The exchange is done without an exam if the licence is valid and you submit the issuing country's confirmation.

What is the fine for driving without a licence?

GEL 500; repeatedly within a year — GEL 1,500 or up to 15 days' administrative detention.

In which languages is the theory test available?

Besides Georgian — English, Russian, Turkish, Azerbaijani, Armenian, Abkhaz and Ossetian.

Can a foreigner open an account at a Georgian bank?

Yes. At some banks a passport and a mobile number are enough, though you often need to visit a branch to complete registration.

Why did the bank refuse me without a reason?

By law a bank must refuse if it can't complete its checks on the customer and the funds, and it doesn't have to explain in detail. Reapplying with the right documents, or at another bank, often succeeds.

Can I open an account without coming to Georgia?

In some cases — through a power of attorney or remote identification, but it depends on the bank. Often you still need to visit once in person.

How much does your service cost?

Our prices for opening accounts are listed on the company registration page.

How much of my deposit is insured?

Up to GEL 50,000 per depositor per bank, rising to GEL 100,000 from 1 January 2027.

Do I become resident automatically after 183 days?

Yes — 183 days in any continuous 12-month period ending in the tax year makes you resident for that whole year. The certificate, however, has to be requested separately.

As a resident, do I pay Georgian tax on foreign income?

No. A resident individual's foreign-source income is exempt in Georgia. Your home country's law may see it differently — we check that separately.

Is deposit interest taxed?

Interest an individual receives from a licensed financial institution, such as a bank, is not taxed and is not included in taxable income.

How long does the certificate take?

Within 30 calendar days. If that isn't possible, the Revenue Service tells you the expected date.

How many double tax treaties does Georgia have?

58 according to the Ministry of Finance, including with every EU country and the UK.

Is there a US–Georgia tax treaty?

Not on Georgia's list, but the IRS considers the 1973 US–USSR treaty to apply to Georgia. In any case, US citizens are taxed by the US on worldwide income.

How do I use a treaty?

You need a residence certificate from the country where you are resident. The Georgian certificate is issued by the Revenue Service within 30 days.

Do I pay tax on income from selling crypto?

As an individual — no: under the Ministry of Finance's 2019 public decision, this income is exempt from personal income tax. Different rules apply to companies.

Can I run a crypto exchange as a sole trader?

No. Only an LLC or JSC can register as a VASP.

How much capital does a VASP need?

From GEL 150,000 to GEL 350,000, depending on the service. Providers registered earlier must meet this by 1 September 2027.

Is information on Georgian companies public?

Yes. Business registry data and copies of registration documents are public; an official extract is issued for a fee.

Can I find out who really owns a company?

The registry shows legal owners. Georgia has no public beneficial-ownership register, so complex structures need further checks.

How long does a check take?

A basic check usually takes a few working days; full due diligence depends on the size of the company.

Can a foreigner buy land in Georgia?

Non-agricultural land — yes. Agricultural land — only through inheritance, or for a company with a foreign dominant partner, under an investment plan and Government decision.

I'm a dual citizen — can I buy agricultural land?

Yes. A dual citizen counts as a Georgian citizen under this law.

Do I pay tax when I sell?

A flat or house you owned for more than 2 years is sold tax-free. Other cases we check individually.

Is there a digital nomad visa in Georgia?

Not a separate one today. Citizens of many countries can stay visa-free for a year; for a longer stay you need a residence permit.

Can I work remotely for a foreign employer while visa-free?

Yes. The Labour Migration Law usually doesn't require the right to work when the work relates to a foreign company's business abroad. Tax, however, is due in Georgia.

Do digital nomads pay tax in Georgia?

Yes, for work done while in Georgia: 20% through an annual return, or 1% as a sole trader where the activity qualifies. A non-resident who works here 30 days or less a year doesn't pay tax on the salary.

Can a foreign salary go through a 1% sole trader?

No. Salary is excluded from the 1%. If you genuinely provide services rather than work as an employee, the contract must show it — we review the contract.

Will I still owe tax in my home country?

Possibly. That depends on your home country's residency rules and on its tax treaty with Georgia. We check both sides.

Does a child born in Georgia become a Georgian citizen?

Not by birth alone. The child is a citizen if one parent is Georgian, or if the parents are stateless persons with status in Georgia.

How much does a birth certificate cost?

The birth certificate is free. Registration is usually done by the next working day.

How do I get the certificate recognised in my country?

It needs an apostille and a translation; then you register the child at your country's consulate.

Do I need the other parent's consent to leave with the child?

For a foreign child, Georgian law doesn't require it unless a court has restricted exit. Your country's rules may differ.

How long can a foreign-registered car stay in Georgia?

90 calendar days. Then it must leave, be cleared or be placed under temporary import — for up to 3 years.

What is the fine for overstaying 90 days?

GEL 50 a day, up to GEL 1,000.

Can I sell my foreign-plated car in Georgia?

Only after customs clearance. Selling or handing it over without clearance is fined 100% of the import duties.

How much is car import tax in Georgia in 2026?

Excise is GEL 1.50 per cm³ for cars up to 6 years old and GEL 4.50 for older ones; right-hand drive pays triple. Import tax is added; there is no VAT.

Yes. Your representative can file the application with the notary under a power of attorney. The key is not to miss the 6-month deadline.
