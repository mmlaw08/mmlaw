## What a civil litigation lawyer does

A civil litigation lawyer assesses your claim and evidence, tries to settle the dispute by negotiation, prepares the lawsuit if needed and represents you at every court instance — through to enforcement of the judgment.

## When you need a civil litigation lawyer

- Someone won't repay a debt

- A contract has been breached

- You suffered damage and want compensation

- You have a property or neighbour dispute

- You've been sued and need to defend yourself

- A court decision isn't being enforced

## Stages of a civil dispute

- Assessing the case and gathering evidence.

- Pre-trial claim and negotiation — many disputes end here.

- Filing the lawsuit with the district (city) court.

- Hearing and judgment.

- Appeal to the Court of Appeals and then to the Supreme Court.

- Enforcement of the judgment through the National Bureau of Enforcement.

## Documents to prepare

- Contracts and annexes

- Correspondence — emails, messages

- Proof of payments and receipts

- Photos, expert reports, certificates

- Letters received from the court or the other side

## Mistakes to avoid

- Relying only on a verbal agreement

- Deleting or not keeping correspondence and receipts

- Missing the limitation period

- Ignoring court notices

- Signing a document from the other side that you don't understand

## Questions & answers

### How are civil courts organised in Georgia?

First instance — district (city) courts; second instance — the Tbilisi and Kutaisi Courts of Appeal; the final, cassation instance — the Supreme Court of Georgia.

### Can a dispute be resolved without going to court?

Yes — through negotiation, mediation or arbitration. For some categories of disputes (for example certain family, inheritance and neighbour disputes) court mediation may be mandatory.

### How long do I have to file a claim?

It depends on the type of claim. Under the Civil Code of Georgia the general limitation period is 10 years, 3 years for contractual claims and 6 years for claims related to immovable property. Some claims have shorter periods, so it is best to act early.
