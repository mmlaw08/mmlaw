## What a family lawyer does

A family lawyer helps with divorce, child residence and alimony, and dividing marital property. We try to reach an agreement first — if that isn't possible, we represent you in court.

## When you need a family lawyer

- Your spouse doesn't agree to the divorce

- You can't agree where the child will live or how the other parent will see them

- You need alimony set, increased or reduced

- Property acquired during the marriage has to be divided

- You want to sign a marriage contract

- Paternity needs to be established

## Divorce in Georgia: two routes

If both spouses agree and there is no dispute about children or property, the divorce can be registered at the Public Service Hall. If one spouse disagrees or there is a dispute, the court decides.

## Documents to prepare

- ID documents of both spouses

- Marriage certificate

- Children's birth certificates

- Property documents (registry extracts, contracts)

- Proof of income — for alimony

## Mistakes to avoid

- Relying on a verbal agreement — it should be put in writing

- Selling joint property while the dispute is ongoing

- Drawing the child into the dispute

- Taking the child abroad without the other parent's consent

- Ignoring court notices

## Questions & answers

### Can we divorce without going to court?

Yes — if both spouses agree and there is no dispute over children or property, the divorce can be registered with the Civil Registry (through the Public Service Hall). Otherwise the divorce is decided by a court.

### How is property divided?

Property acquired during the marriage is the spouses' joint property and is, as a rule, divided equally unless a marriage contract says otherwise. Property owned before marriage, gifts and inheritance remain personal property.

### How is alimony determined?

Parents must support their minor children. The amount depends on the child's needs and the parents' financial situation — set by agreement or by the court.
