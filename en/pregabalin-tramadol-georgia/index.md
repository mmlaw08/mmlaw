# Lyrica (pregabalin) and tramadol in Georgia — Article 261

In Georgia, Lyrica (pregabalin) and tramadol are psychotropic and potent substances. Obtaining or keeping them without a prescription is a crime (Article 261), and selling them is far more serious. We defend the accused from the forensic examination to the courtroom.

The amounts are set in an annex to the law. Pregabalin: small amount up to 0.6 g, criminal-liability threshold up to 6 g, large amount up to 20 g. Tramadol: small up to 1 g, threshold up to 80 g, large up to 800 g. For pregabalin, tianeptine and ketamine the medicine form itself counts, not just the pure substance — so a few capsules can be enough.
Selling gabapentin, baclofen or cyclodol in any form is a crime (Article 260³).

## Questions & answers

### How much Lyrica is a crime?

More than 0.6 g of pregabalin is already a criminal case; a large amount is over 6 g. The medicine form counts, so a few capsules can be enough.

### I have a prescription — is it still a crime?

Medicine prescribed by a doctor and lawfully obtained is not a crime. Keep the prescription and proof of purchase.

### What is the penalty for tramadol?

Basic — a fine or corrective labour up to 1 year; in a large amount — 3 to 7 years; especially large — 7 to 12 years.
