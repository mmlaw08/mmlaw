# Crimes and penalties in Georgia

Short and clear: what each crime means, which article of the Criminal Code covers it, what the penalty is and what to do if you are accused or are a victim.

## Property crimes

- [Theft](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/theft-article-177/) — Article 177 Secretly taking someone else's property. Penalties range from a fine to 10 years, depending on the damage and circumstances.

- [Robbery (open theft)](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/robbery-article-178/) — Article 178 Openly taking someone else's property. Penalties range from a fine to 11 years, depending on the damage, violence and other factors.

- [Violent robbery](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/violent-robbery-article-179/) — Article 179 An attack to take property, with violence dangerous to life or health or the threat of it. Penalty: 5 to 15 years.

- [Extortion](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/extortion-article-181/) — Article 181 Demanding money or property under threat of violence, damage or spreading compromising information. Up to 9 years.

- [Embezzlement](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/embezzlement-article-182/) — Article 182 Unlawfully keeping or spending property or money you were entrusted with — often at work. Up to 11 years.

- [Damage to property](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/property-damage-article-187/) — Article 187 Damaging or destroying someone else's property causing significant damage. Penalties from a fine to 12 years.

## Violent crimes

- [Bodily harm](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/bodily-harm/) — Article 117, 118, 120 Intentional minor, less serious and serious bodily harm — penalties from a fine to 14 years.

- [Hooliganism](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/hooliganism-article-239/) — Article 239 A gross breach of public order with violence or the threat of violence. Penalties from a fine to 7 years.

- [Domestic violence](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/domestic-violence-article-126-1/) — Article 126¹ Violence, systematic insults, blackmail or humiliation of a family member. Up to 3 years and a restraining order.

## Cybercrime and privacy

- [Unauthorised access to a computer system](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/hacking-article-284/) — Article 284 Unlawfully getting into someone else's account, email, phone or computer. From a fine to 6 years.

- [Computer crime for financial gain](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/phishing-online-theft-article-286-1/) — Article 286¹ Phishing, taking money with someone else's card or online banking, interfering with systems for money. Up to 11 years.

- [Violation of private life](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/private-photos-article-157/) — Article 157, 157¹ Unlawfully obtaining and spreading private information, photos or personal data. Up to 10 years.

## Traffic crimes

- [Traffic accident causing injury](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/traffic-accident-article-276/) — Article 276 Breaking traffic safety rules causing injury or death; driving under the influence of drugs. Up to 12 years.

## Corruption crimes

- [Bribe-taking](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/bribe-taking-article-338/) — Article 338 An official taking or demanding money or another benefit for an official act. Penalty: 6 to 15 years.

- [Bribe-giving](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/bribe-giving-article-339/) — Article 339 Offering or giving money or a benefit to an official. From a fine to 8 years; a voluntary report releases from liability.

- [Trading in influence](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/trading-in-influence-article-339-1/) — Article 339¹ Selling or buying 'connections' — promising, for money, to influence an official's decision. Up to 7 years.

- [Accepting a prohibited gift](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/prohibited-gift-article-340/) — Article 340 An official accepting a gift the law prohibits. From a fine to 4 years in prison.

- [Official forgery](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/official-forgery-article-341/) — Article 341 An official making a false entry in an official document or issuing a false document. From a fine to 4 years.

## Administrative offences

- [Disobeying the police](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/disobeying-police-article-173/) — Article 173 Disobeying a lawful police demand or insulting an officer: a fine up to GEL 5,000 or up to 60 days of administrative detention.

- [Petty hooliganism](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/petty-hooliganism-article-166/) — Article 166 Swearing and insulting people in public: a fine up to GEL 3,000 or up to 20 days of administrative detention.
