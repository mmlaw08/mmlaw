# Georgian citizenship for foreigners — how to get it

A foreigner can become a Georgian citizen in several ways — through residence, marriage, birth in Georgia or an exceptional grant. The President of Georgia decides; applications go to the Public Service Development Agency or a consulate. We choose the right route for you, prepare the documents and defend your interests if you are refused.

Since a 2025 amendment, the ordinary route requires lawful, continuous residence in Georgia for the last 10 years (it used to be 5). Residence stops being continuous if you spend more than 90 days in total abroad in a year — study (up to 2 years) and medical treatment don't count.
Georgia allows dual citizenship only as an exception. For ordinary or marriage-based grants, the decree takes effect only once you prove you have given up your other citizenship. Keeping your own citizenship is possible with an exceptional grant.

## How the process works

- The language, history and basic law tests are taken before applying; a pass is valid for 1 year.

- You apply to the Public Service Development Agency or a Georgian consulate abroad — in person or through a representative.

- The Agency and the Citizenship Commission review the application and the President decides — within 3 months.

- On being granted citizenship, you take an oath of loyalty in Georgian.

## When applications are refused

Grounds for refusal include involvement in international or especially grave crimes, state security interests, being wanted, or extradition proceedings. After a refusal you can reapply on the same ground after 6 months. Following a 2025 Constitutional Court decision, a refusal can also be challenged in court.

## Questions & answers

### How many years must I live in Georgia to get citizenship?

By the ordinary route — the last 10 years, lawfully and continuously. The spouse of a Georgian citizen — 5 years. A stateless person with status — 5 years.

### Can I keep my own citizenship (dual citizenship)?

Only with an exceptional grant. Through the ordinary route or marriage you must give up your previous citizenship.

### My father is Georgian — am I entitled to citizenship?

If a parent was a Georgian citizen when you were born, you are a citizen by birth. For adults, Georgian roots count only for an exceptional grant.

### I was born in Georgia in the Soviet era — what can I get?

Special rules apply to people born in Georgia, but only until 1 January 2027. The conditions depend on your date of birth, your situation in 1993 and whether you hold another citizenship — contact us in good time.

### How long does the decision take?

By law — 3 months. The President makes the decision.
