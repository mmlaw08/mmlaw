Criminal defense lawyer for foreigners

When a foreigner faces a criminal case in Georgia, language and unfamiliar procedure are the first obstacles. We defend you in English or Russian — from the moment of arrest to trial.

If you are detained, you have the right to a lawyer, to a free interpreter and to have your country's consulate informed. Do not sign documents you don't understand — ask for your lawyer first.

We act at every stage: questioning, preventive measures (including bail or detention), plea agreements, trial and appeal. We also keep family members abroad informed.

  • Challenging bail or detention

Questions & answers

I'm a foreigner and was detained in Georgia. What are my rights?

You have the right to remain silent, to a lawyer before any questioning, to a free interpreter and to have your consulate informed. Ask for a lawyer immediately and don't sign anything you don't understand.

Will my consulate be informed?

Yes, if you ask. A detained foreigner has the right to request that the consulate of their country be informed of the detention.

Can my family hire a lawyer from abroad?

Yes. A family member can contact us by phone or WhatsApp; we sign the engagement remotely and step into the case immediately.

Legal basis

  • Constitution of Georgia
  • Criminal Procedure Code of Georgia
