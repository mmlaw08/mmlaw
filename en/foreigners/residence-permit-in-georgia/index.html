Residence permit in Georgia — lawyer for foreigners

We prepare and file residence permit applications for foreigners in Georgia, follow them through the Public Service Hall and appeal refusals.

Request a consultation+995 551 00 43 00

Citizens of many countries can stay in Georgia visa-free for up to a year, but living, working or doing business here long-term usually requires a residence permit. The right type depends on your situation: work, business, property ownership, family, study or investment.

Refusals often come with little explanation. A carefully prepared application avoids most of them — and if you are refused, the decision can be challenged within short deadlines.

What we handle

  • Choosing the right type of residence permit
  • Preparing and filing the application
  • Work and business residence permits
  • Permits based on property or investment
  • Family reunification
  • Appeals against refusals
Residence permits

Questions & answers

How long does a residence permit application take?

The standard review takes up to about 30 days; an expedited review is available for an extra fee. We tell you the current timelines when we prepare your application.

Can a refusal be appealed?

Yes. A refusal is an administrative decision and can be challenged — first administratively, then in court. Deadlines are short (usually one month), so contact a lawyer as soon as you receive it.

Do I need a work permit to work in Georgia?

Labour-migration rules have been tightened in recent years, and foreigners working in Georgia may need a work permit or to register their employment. We check what applies to you before you start work.
