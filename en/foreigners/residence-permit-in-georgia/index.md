# Residence permit in Georgia — lawyer for foreigners

We prepare and file residence permit applications for foreigners in Georgia, follow them through the Public Service Hall and appeal refusals.

- Confidential

- Georgian · English · Russian

- Free first consultation

- Courts of all instances

Citizens of many countries can stay in Georgia visa-free for up to a year, but living, working or doing business here long-term usually requires a residence permit. The right type depends on your situation: work, business, property ownership, family, study or investment.
Refusals often come with little explanation. A carefully prepared application avoids most of them — and if you are refused, the decision can be challenged within short deadlines.

## Renewal — deadlines

- Apply to renew no earlier than 90 days before expiry

- Apply for a new permit at least 40 days before your lawful stay ends

- Staying on an expired permit is an overstay — a fine, and if unpaid, a risk of being refused entry

- If you are abroad, you can apply to renew online

## Questions & answers

### How long does a residence permit application take?

Standard — 30 days (300 GEL). Expedited — 20 days (450 GEL) or 10 days (600 GEL). An application by a Georgian citizen's spouse — up to 90 days.

### Can a refusal be appealed?

Yes — in court, within 10 calendar days of receiving the decision. The deadline is very short, so contact us as soon as you are refused.

### Do I need a work permit to work in Georgia?

Since 1 March 2026, foreigners need the right to labour activity to work or do business in Georgia — except holders of a permanent residence permit and other cases set by law. We'll check what applies to you before you start work.

### How long can I stay without a visa?

Citizens of listed countries — a full year; others — 90 days in any 180. The old 'Remotely from Georgia' digital-nomad programme is not currently active — for a longer stay you need a residence permit.
