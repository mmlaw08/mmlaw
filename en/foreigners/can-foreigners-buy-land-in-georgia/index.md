# Can foreigners buy land in Georgia? Agricultural land

Foreigners can buy flats, houses and non-agricultural land in Georgia. Agricultural land, under the Constitution, may belong only to the state, municipalities and Georgian citizens — with narrow exceptions. We check the land category, choose a lawful route and protect your investment.

Under Article 19(4) of the Constitution, agricultural land may be owned only by the state, a self-governing unit, a Georgian citizen or a union of Georgian citizens. Exceptions are possible only by organic law.
Under the 2019 Organic Law, a foreigner may own agricultural land only through inheritance. A company registered in Georgia whose dominant (over 50%) partner is a foreigner may acquire it only under an investment plan, by Government decision. A dual citizen counts as a Georgian citizen for this law.

## What to know

- Before buying, check the land category on the Public Registry extract — agricultural or non-agricultural

- If investment-plan obligations aren't met, the land must be sold within 1 year or it passes to the state

- A mortgage over agricultural land with transfer to a foreign creditor is banned, as is a change of partners that makes a foreigner the dominant partner

- Financial institutions designated by the Government are exempt

- When agricultural land is sold, co-owners and registered lessees have a pre-emption right — foreigners don't

## Tax on selling property

An individual selling a flat or house (with its land) owned for more than 2 years pays no tax. If owned for less, the gain is taxed at 5%. Other assets, such as a plot of land, are also exempt after 2 years if not used in business.

## Questions & answers

### Can a foreigner buy land in Georgia?

Non-agricultural land — yes. Agricultural land — only through inheritance, or for a company with a foreign dominant partner, under an investment plan and Government decision.

### I'm a dual citizen — can I buy agricultural land?

Yes. A dual citizen counts as a Georgian citizen under this law.

### Do I pay tax when I sell?

A flat or house you owned for more than 2 years is sold tax-free. Other cases we check individually.
