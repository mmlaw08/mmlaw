# Disputes with property developers in Georgia

When you buy a flat in a new build, the biggest risk is the developer. We help buyers — including foreigners — get their flat or their money back, through negotiation or in court.

- Confidential

- Georgian · English · Russian

- Free first consultation

- Courts of all instances

Typical problems are missed completion dates, a flat that differs from the contract, a mortgage on the land or the project, the same flat sold to several people, and an insolvent developer. Each needs a different approach.
First we review the contract, the payments, the Public Registry records and the building permits. Then we choose the strategy: negotiation, a claim for the contractual penalty and damages, terminating the contract and recovering the money, securing the claim — for example, by freezing property — or filing a claim in the developer's insolvency.

## Our services

- Checking the contract and the developer before you sign

- Claiming the penalty when completion is late

- Terminating the contract and recovering your money

- Disputes over a mortgage or seizure on the flat or land

- Disputes when one flat was sold to two buyers

- Claims when the developer is insolvent

## Questions & answers

### The developer is late — what can I do?

Depending on the contract, you can claim the penalty, damages, or terminate the contract and get your money back. We start by reviewing the contract.

### Can you handle this while I live abroad?

Yes. We act for you under a power of attorney — in negotiations and in court.

### What should I do before paying for a flat?

Check the developer, the landowner, mortgages and permits, and have a lawyer read the contract. It is the cheapest protection there is.
