# Work permit and sole trader status for foreigners in Georgia

Since 1 March 2026, foreigners need the right to labour activity to work or do business in Georgia. We check whether it applies to you and sort out your status — from the permit to your taxes.

The rule covers both employees and the self-employed — including individual entrepreneurs. Along with the right to work you need a suitable residence permit or a D1 visa, and you can only apply while you are lawfully in Georgia. Foreigners with a permanent residence permit are not covered.
With small business status, tax is 1% of income if annual income does not exceed GEL 500,000. But not every activity qualifies, and working without the right to work can even be a ground for deportation — so we plan everything together.

## Questions & answers

### Do individual entrepreneurs need the right to work?

Yes. A self-employed foreigner, including an individual entrepreneur, needs it to work in Georgia.

### Who is not covered by the rule?

For example, foreigners with a permanent residence permit. We check other exemptions for your case.

### What happens if annual income exceeds GEL 500,000?

From the start of that month until the end of the year, all income is taxed at 3%.
