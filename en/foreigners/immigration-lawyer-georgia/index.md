# Immigration lawyer in Georgia

When you are turned away at the border, have overstayed or face deportation, time is very short. We act from day one — in English or Russian, in Georgia or remotely.

- Confidential

- Georgian · English · Russian

- Free first consultation

- Courts of all instances

In immigration cases deadlines are counted in days: a deportation decision can be appealed in court within 10 days of being served. So the first step is to get a copy of the decision and understand exactly what it is based on.
Often an appeal is not the only answer: we look for a lawful basis to stay — the right type of residence permit, a family tie or another route — and at the same time protect your property, job and family interests.

## Questions & answers

### How many days do I have to appeal a deportation decision?

10 days from when it is served. Contact a lawyer right away.

### Can you handle my case if I'm already abroad?

Yes. We act under a power of attorney and keep you informed at every step.

### Does paying the overstay fine cancel the entry ban?

No. For an overstay, the fine and the entry ban are imposed together.
