# Crypto in Georgia — tax and VASP registration

In Georgia, income an individual receives from supplying crypto assets is exempt from personal income tax — as the Ministry of Finance has ruled. A crypto business — exchange, conversion, transfers, custody — may operate only after registering with the National Bank. Under the Ministry of Finance's Public Decision No. 201 of 2019, crypto assets have no location, so an individual's income from supplying them is not Georgian-source income and is exempt from personal income tax. Exchanging crypto for money is not subject to VAT. A public decision binds the Revenue Service until the underlying rules change.
A company's profits from crypto, however, are taxed under the usual rules. And mind your home country's law — if you are resident there, you may owe tax there.

## VASP registration with the National Bank

- Only an LLC or JSC registered in Georgia can register

- Registration fee — GEL 5,000

- Minimum capital: GEL 150,000 — exchange or transfers; GEL 250,000 — other services; GEL 350,000 — a trading platform

- The National Bank decides within 60 calendar days (which can be extended)

- Lending virtual assets to individuals is banned, and so are privacy coins

- A VASP is an obliged entity under the AML law: customer identification and automated transaction monitoring are mandatory

## Questions & answers

### Do I pay tax on income from selling crypto?

As an individual — no: under the Ministry of Finance's 2019 public decision, this income is exempt from personal income tax. Different rules apply to companies.

### Can I run a crypto exchange as a sole trader?

No. Only an LLC or JSC can register as a VASP.

### How much capital does a VASP need?

From GEL 150,000 to GEL 350,000, depending on the service. Providers registered earlier must meet this by 1 September 2027.
