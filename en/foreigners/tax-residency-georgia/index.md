# Tax residency in Georgia — 183 days, certificate, HNWI

You become a Georgian tax resident if you spend 183 days or more in Georgia in any continuous 12-month period. A resident individual's foreign-source income is not taxed in Georgia. We help confirm residency, obtain the certificate, apply for HNWI status and handle disputes with the Revenue Service.

Under Article 34 of the Tax Code, a person is resident for the whole tax year if they are in Georgia 183 days or more in any continuous 12 calendar months ending in that year. Any part of a day counts as a full day. Time abroad for treatment, holidays, business trips or study counts as time in Georgia. The status is determined separately for each year.
A resident individual's income from sources outside Georgia is exempt (Article 82(1)(f)). Income earned in Georgia is generally taxed at 20%, dividends at 5%, and interest from a licensed bank is not taxed (Article 131(5)).

## The residence certificate

- The Revenue Service issues it — you can also request it online

- Decision or certificate within 30 calendar days; up to 30 days to fix any defects

- On request, the Revenue Service sends the certificate directly to the foreign tax authority and signs the other country's form

- You need it for treaty relief, for banks and for CRS purposes

## High-net-worth individual (HNWI)

Residency can also be granted without 183 days to a high-net-worth individual (Article 34(6); Finance Minister Order No. 60 of 2023). The main conditions are assets above GEL 3,000,000 or income above GEL 200,000 in each of the last 3 years, property in Georgia worth at least USD 500,000 and, in addition, a residence permit or at least GEL 25,000 of Georgian-source income in the previous year. The status must be applied for again each tax year.

## Questions & answers

### Do I become resident automatically after 183 days?

Yes — 183 days in any continuous 12-month period ending in the tax year makes you resident for that whole year. The certificate, however, has to be requested separately.

### As a resident, do I pay Georgian tax on foreign income?

No. A resident individual's foreign-source income is exempt in Georgia. Your home country's law may see it differently — we check that separately.

### Is deposit interest taxed?

Interest an individual receives from a licensed financial institution, such as a bank, is not taxed and is not included in taxable income.

### How long does the certificate take?

Within 30 calendar days. If that isn't possible, the Revenue Service tells you the expected date.
