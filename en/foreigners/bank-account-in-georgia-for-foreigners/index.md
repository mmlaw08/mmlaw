# Opening a bank account in Georgia as a foreigner

A foreigner can open an account at a Georgian bank — sometimes with just a passport. But the bank must understand who you are and where your money comes from, and if it can't, it won't open the account. We prepare the documents, help you deal with the bank and open personal and business accounts.

Under Article 10 of the anti-money-laundering law, a bank must identify and verify the customer and the beneficial owner, the purpose of the relationship and the expected transactions. If it can't, opening the account is prohibited. For high-risk customers the bank also looks into the origin of funds.
That is why refusals often come without an explanation — and that is lawful. We can't promise that a bank will open an account — that is the bank's decision — but properly prepared documents greatly reduce the risk of refusal.

## What to expect

- Usually a passport and a Georgian mobile number; to complete registration you often need to visit a branch

- The bank may ask for documents on the source of income, your business and the purpose of the account

- Some products are available only to citizens of certain countries

- To open a business account you usually need a personal account first

- Remote identification is allowed by law, but not every bank offers it to every customer

## How to reduce the risk of refusal

- Prepare proof of income — a contract, statements, a tax return.

- Explain clearly what you need the account for and what transactions you expect.

- For a business account, prepare the company documents and beneficial-owner information.

- If one bank refuses, another may assess you differently.

## Questions & answers

### Can a foreigner open an account at a Georgian bank?

Yes. At some banks a passport and a mobile number are enough, though you often need to visit a branch to complete registration.

### Why did the bank refuse me without a reason?

By law a bank must refuse if it can't complete its checks on the customer and the funds, and it doesn't have to explain in detail. Reapplying with the right documents, or at another bank, often succeeds.

### Can I open an account without coming to Georgia?

In some cases — through a power of attorney or remote identification, but it depends on the bank. Often you still need to visit once in person.
