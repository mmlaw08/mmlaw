Power of attorney from abroad

A power of attorney lets your lawyer act on your behalf in Georgia. It can be issued in three ways:

01

At a Georgian embassy or consulate

Georgian consular offices perform notarial acts. Such a power of attorney needs no further certification.

02

With a local notary

Depending on the country, the power of attorney may need an apostille or consular legalisation — documents from some countries need neither. A certified Georgian translation is required.

03

Online with a notary in Georgia

If you have a Georgian passport, you can sign the power of attorney with a notary in Georgia online — by video call, with two witnesses present at the notary's office.

We prepare the text so it covers exactly the powers needed — so you won't have to issue it twice.

The most common cases

Inheritance: the 6-month deadline

An inheritance must be accepted within 6 months of its opening — as a rule, the date of death. The application to the notary can be filed through a representative. If you missed the deadline for a valid reason, a court can restore it.

Selling property remotely

Your lawyer checks the buyer and the contract, completes the transaction and registers it with the Public Registry. We agree a safe way to receive the money in advance.

Divorced abroad

If you divorced in another country, the divorce generally has to be recognised in Georgia — this is handled by the Supreme Court of Georgia. If you need a divorce in Georgia, we represent you in court.

Frequently asked questions

Can I accept an inheritance in Georgia without coming?

Yes. Your representative can file the application with the notary under a power of attorney. The key is not to miss the 6-month deadline.

Where do I sign a power of attorney?

At a Georgian embassy or consulate; with a local notary — depending on the country, an apostille or legalisation and a certified translation may be needed; or, if you have a Georgian passport, online with a notary in Georgia by video call, with two witnesses present. We prepare the text for you.

What if the 6-month deadline has passed?

If you missed it for a valid reason, a court can extend it. Whether you actually took possession of the property also matters. We'll assess your case.

Can I sell an apartment by power of attorney?

Yes. A lawyer or trusted person signs the sale and registers it with the Public Registry under your power of attorney. We check that the deal is safe.

Do I need to come to Georgia for a divorce?

Usually not — your lawyer can represent you in court under a power of attorney. If you divorced abroad, the decision may need to be recognised in Georgia.
