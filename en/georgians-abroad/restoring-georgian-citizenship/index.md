# Restoring and keeping Georgian citizenship

Did you lose Georgian citizenship when you became a citizen of another country? The law gives a temporary chance to get it back until 1 January 2027 — without giving up your other citizenship.

- Confidential

- Georgian · English · Russian

- Free first consultation

- Courts of all instances

Under Article 32² of the Organic Law on Georgian Citizenship, a former citizen who lost Georgian citizenship because they acquired another one can apply to the Public Service Development Agency until 1 January 2027. The conditions are knowledge of the state language to the set level and the absence of legal obstacles set by the law. Under this route you don't have to give up your foreign citizenship, and your minor children are granted citizenship too.
If you are still a Georgian citizen and are about to acquire another citizenship, you must get consent to keep Georgian citizenship before you acquire the other one. We check your status, prepare the application and documents, and appeal a refusal.

## Questions & answers

### Will I have to give up my other citizenship?

Not when citizenship is restored under Article 32². This option is open until 1 January 2027.

### What are the conditions?

Knowledge of the state language to the set level and no legal obstacles under the law. The language requirement does not apply to some people with serious health conditions.

### Will my child get it too?

Yes — your minor children are granted citizenship too; a child aged 14 or older, with their consent.
