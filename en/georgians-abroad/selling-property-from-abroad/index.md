# Selling or buying property in Georgia from abroad

Living abroad and need to sell, buy or rent out property in Georgia? We do it in your name — so that your money and your property stay protected.

- Confidential

- Georgian · English · Russian

- Free first consultation

- Courts of all instances

Your lawyer checks the buyer or seller, the property's history, mortgages and seizures, prepares the contract, completes the transaction and registers it with the Public Registry. We agree a safe way to receive the money in advance.
You can give the power of attorney at a Georgian consulate, with a local notary or — if you have a Georgian passport — online with a notary in Georgia. We draft it to cover only the powers that are needed.

- Fixing errors in the registry

## Questions & answers

### Can I sell a flat without coming to Georgia?

Yes — with a properly drafted power of attorney, a lawyer completes the deal in your name.

### How do I receive the money safely?

We agree the route in advance — for example, a transfer straight to your own bank account. This should also be written into the power of attorney.

### Can I give a power of attorney online?

Yes, if you have a Georgian passport — with a notary in Georgia by video call, with two witnesses present.
