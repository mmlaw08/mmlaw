# Inherit in Georgia from abroad without travelling

A family member has died in Georgia and you live abroad? You can accept the inheritance without coming — the key is not to miss the 6-month deadline.

- Confidential

- Georgian · English · Russian

- Free first consultation

- Courts of all instances

An inheritance must be accepted within 6 months of its opening — as a rule, the date of death. The application to the notary can be filed through a representative. If you missed the deadline for a valid reason, a court can restore it.
We prepare the power of attorney text, gather the documents, obtain the certificate of inheritance and register the property in your name with the Public Registry. If the heirs disagree, we represent you in court.

## Questions & answers

### How long do I have to accept an inheritance?

6 months from its opening — as a rule, the date of death.

### Do I need to come to Georgia?

No. The application to the notary can be filed through a representative under a power of attorney.

### I missed the deadline — is it too late?

Not necessarily. If you had a valid reason, a court can restore the deadline.
