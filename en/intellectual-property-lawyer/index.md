## Registering a trademark, step by step

- Check that no similar mark is already registered.

- Choose the classes of goods and services.

- File the application with Sakpatenti.

- Go through examination and obtain registration.

- Monitor the market and renew the registration on time.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Using a brand without checking it first

- Registering only the domain, not the trademark

- Having no written agreement transferring rights from your designer or developer

## Questions & answers

### Where are trademarks registered in Georgia?

With Sakpatenti — the National Intellectual Property Center of Georgia. International registration is possible through the Madrid system.

### How long does trademark protection last?

10 years from the filing date, renewable every 10 years.

### Does copyright need to be registered?

No — copyright arises automatically when a work is created. Depositing the work with Sakpatenti helps prove authorship.
