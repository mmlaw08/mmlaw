# Debt recovery in Georgia — loans, IOUs, enforcement

A debt can be recovered through the courts or — if the contract was certified by a notary — even without going to court. We assess your documents, choose the fastest route and make sure the debtor doesn't dispose of their assets.

The limitation period for a claim to repay a loan is 3 years (Civil Code, Article 129) — usually from the day the money was due. So don't wait: once it has passed, the debtor can argue that the claim is time-barred.
The law doesn't always require a loan agreement to be in writing, but in a dispute documents decide: the contract, an IOU, bank transfers, messages. The effective annual interest rate on a loan must not exceed 50% — this applies to every loan, including between private individuals.

## Recovering a debt step by step

- We gather evidence — the contract, IOU, transfers, messages, witnesses.

- We send the debtor a written demand with a deadline — often this alone is enough.

- If they don't pay, we file a lawsuit. The court fee is 3% of the amount claimed, but at least 100 GEL.

- Together with the lawsuit we can ask the court to freeze the debtor's property or account so it isn't disposed of before the case is decided.

- After the judgment, the National Bureau of Enforcement collects the debt and the debtor is entered into the debtors register.

## When you can skip the court

If the loan agreement was certified by a notary and the parties agreed in it to the issue of a writ of execution, once the debt is due the notary issues the writ (Law on Notaries, Article 38) and enforcement starts without a court case. That is why we recommend signing larger loans before a notary.
A claim based on a promissory note can be heard by the court under a simplified procedure — only documents and the parties' explanations are used as evidence.

## Questions & answers

### I lent money without an IOU — can I get it back?

Possibly. Evidence can be a bank transfer, messages, an audio recording or a witness. The sooner you contact us, the more evidence we can preserve.

### How long do I have to claim the debt?

The limitation period is 3 years — usually from the repayment date. After that the debtor can rely on the limitation period and the court will reject the claim.

### How much does a lawsuit cost?

The court fee is 3% of the amount claimed, but at least 100 GEL. If you win, the losing party usually reimburses the court costs.

### The debtor has no assets — what happens?

The judgment goes to enforcement and the debtor is entered into the debtors register: they can't register new property or a car without the enforcement bureau finding out. Money can also be deducted from their salary.
