## What a corporate lawyer does

A corporate lawyer helps you set up a company, draft the charter and shareholder agreement, prepare and review contracts and reduce your business's legal risks.

## Registering an LLC, step by step

- Choose the legal form and company name.

- Prepare the charter and, if needed, a shareholder agreement.

- Secure a legal address in Georgia.

- Register the company with the Public Registry — it is registered as a taxpayer at the same time.

- Open a bank account.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Working without an agreement between partners

- Leaving share transfers and exit rules out of the charter

- Mixing personal and company money

- Choosing the wrong tax regime

- Signing contracts without legal review

## Questions & answers

### How long does it take to register a company in Georgia?

With properly prepared documents, registering an LLC with the National Agency of Public Registry usually takes one business day.

### Can a foreigner own 100% of a Georgian company?

Yes. Foreign individuals and companies can be the sole founders of a Georgian LLC.

### Which law governs companies in Georgia?

The Law of Georgia on Entrepreneurs (new version in force since 2022).
