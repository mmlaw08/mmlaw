## What a criminal defense lawyer does

A criminal defense lawyer protects your rights at every stage of a criminal case: assists you during questioning, attends investigative actions, gathers evidence for the defense, challenges unlawful decisions and defends you in court. The goal is a fair outcome — with your rights respected at every step.

## When you need a criminal defense lawyer

- When you are summoned for questioning — as a witness or an accused

- When your home or workplace is being searched

- When you or a relative has been arrested

- When you have been charged or offered a plea agreement

- When you are a victim of a crime and want your interests protected

## Stages of a criminal case in Georgia

- Arrest and charge — a person must be charged within 48 hours of arrest and brought before a court within the following 24 hours.

- First appearance hearing — the court decides on a preventive measure (for example bail or detention).

- Pre-trial hearing — the court decides which evidence will be admitted at trial.

- Trial — the court hears both sides, examines the evidence and delivers a verdict.

- Appeal — the verdict can be appealed to the Court of Appeals and then, by cassation, to the Supreme Court.

Involving a lawyer as early as possible — ideally from the first contact with the investigating authority — often decides the outcome.

## Questions & answers

### When do I have the right to a lawyer in Georgia?

From the moment of arrest or of being charged — and before any questioning. You have the right to remain silent and to insist on your lawyer being present.

### How long can I be held without a court decision?

A person must be charged within 48 hours of arrest and brought before a court within the following 24 hours. If this doesn't happen, they must be released immediately.

### What is a plea agreement?

An agreement between the prosecution and the accused on guilt and sentence, which must be approved by the court. A defense lawyer's participation is mandatory. Never sign anything without independent legal advice.

### When should I contact a criminal defense lawyer?

As early as possible — ideally as soon as you are summoned for questioning, or during a search or arrest. Involving a lawyer early often decides the outcome: your lawyer helps you avoid mistakes when giving statements and protects your rights while evidence is gathered.
