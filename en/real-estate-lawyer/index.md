## What a real estate lawyer does

A real estate lawyer checks the property and the seller, drafts and reviews contracts, completes the transaction and registration with the Public Registry, and represents you in court if a dispute arises.

## Buying property, step by step

- Get a Public Registry extract — check the owner, mortgage, seizures and prohibitions.

- Check the seller's authority and, for new builds, the developer and permits.

- If needed, sign a preliminary agreement with clear terms on the deposit.

- Sign the sale contract and pay through a safe channel.

- Register ownership with the Public Registry.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Paying a deposit before checking the property

- Trusting a developer without checking permits

- Paying cash without proof of payment

- Not checking utility and other debts

- Signing a contract in a language you don't understand

## Questions & answers

### Where is property ownership registered?

At the National Agency of Public Registry (NAPR). Ownership of real estate arises upon registration. A registry extract shows the owner and any encumbrances (mortgage, seizure).

### Can foreigners buy property in Georgia?

Yes. Foreigners can freely buy apartments, houses and non-agricultural land. Purchase of agricultural land by foreigners is restricted by law.

### What should I check before buying?

The registry extract (owner, mortgage, seizure, prohibitions), the seller's authority, permits for new construction, and any utility or other debts.
