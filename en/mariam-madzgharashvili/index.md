# Mariam Madzgharashvili

Lawyer specialising in criminal law since 2019, Doctor of Law. Member of the Georgian Bar Association's Jury Trial Committee and a mentor to young lawyers.

## About me

Criminal law is my main practice: I defend suspects, accused and convicted persons, witnesses and victims at every stage — from the first interview to the Supreme Court, including jury trials. I am also specialised in juvenile justice and children's rights.
I am a mentor in the Georgian Bar Association's Professional Adaptation Programme, training new lawyers. I work in Georgian, English and Russian, so I often defend foreigners and their families.
