- [Crimes and penalties](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/)Short and clear: what each crime means, which article of the Criminal Code covers it, what the penalty is and what to do if you are accused or are a victim.

- [Theft — Article 177](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/theft-article-177/)Secretly taking someone else's property. Penalties range from a fine to 10 years, depending on the damage and circumstances.

- [Robbery (open theft) — Article 178](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/robbery-article-178/)Openly taking someone else's property. Penalties range from a fine to 11 years, depending on the damage, violence and other factors.

- [Violent robbery — Article 179](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/violent-robbery-article-179/)An attack to take property, with violence dangerous to life or health or the threat of it. Penalty: 5 to 15 years.

- [Extortion — Article 181](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/extortion-article-181/)Demanding money or property under threat of violence, damage or spreading compromising information. Up to 9 years.

- [Embezzlement — Article 182](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/embezzlement-article-182/)Unlawfully keeping or spending property or money you were entrusted with — often at work. Up to 11 years.

- [Damage to property — Article 187](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/property-damage-article-187/)Damaging or destroying someone else's property causing significant damage. Penalties from a fine to 12 years.

- [Bodily harm — Article 117, 118, 120](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/bodily-harm/)Intentional minor, less serious and serious bodily harm — penalties from a fine to 14 years.

- [Hooliganism — Article 239](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/hooliganism-article-239/)A gross breach of public order with violence or the threat of violence. Penalties from a fine to 7 years.

- [Traffic accident causing injury — Article 276](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/traffic-accident-article-276/)Breaking traffic safety rules causing injury or death; driving under the influence of drugs. Up to 12 years.

- [Bribe-taking — Article 338](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/bribe-taking-article-338/)An official taking or demanding money or another benefit for an official act. Penalty: 6 to 15 years.

- [Bribe-giving — Article 339](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/bribe-giving-article-339/)Offering or giving money or a benefit to an official. From a fine to 8 years; a voluntary report releases from liability.

- [Trading in influence — Article 339¹](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/trading-in-influence-article-339-1/)Selling or buying 'connections' — promising, for money, to influence an official's decision. Up to 7 years.

- [Accepting a prohibited gift — Article 340](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/prohibited-gift-article-340/)An official accepting a gift the law prohibits. From a fine to 4 years in prison.

- [Official forgery — Article 341](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/official-forgery-article-341/)An official making a false entry in an official document or issuing a false document. From a fine to 4 years.

- [Disobeying the police — Article 173](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/disobeying-police-article-173/)Disobeying a lawful police demand or insulting an officer: a fine up to GEL 5,000 or up to 60 days of administrative detention.

- [Petty hooliganism — Article 166](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/petty-hooliganism-article-166/)Swearing and insulting people in public: a fine up to GEL 3,000 or up to 20 days of administrative detention.

- [Unauthorised access to a computer system — Article 284](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/hacking-article-284/)Unlawfully getting into someone else's account, email, phone or computer. From a fine to 6 years.

- [Computer crime for financial gain — Article 286¹](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/phishing-online-theft-article-286-1/)Phishing, taking money with someone else's card or online banking, interfering with systems for money. Up to 11 years.

- [Violation of private life — Article 157, 157¹](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/private-photos-article-157/)Unlawfully obtaining and spreading private information, photos or personal data. Up to 10 years.

- [Domestic violence — Article 126¹](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/domestic-violence-article-126-1/)Violence, systematic insults, blackmail or humiliation of a family member. Up to 3 years and a restraining order.

- [Murder — Article 108](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/murder-article-108-109/)Intentional murder, murder with aggravating circumstances, attempted murder and causing death by negligence. Up to life imprisonment.

- [Illegal firearms — Article 236](https://mmlaw.ge/en/crimes/illegal-firearms-article-236/)Illegally acquiring, keeping, carrying or selling a firearm, ammunition or explosives. Up to 11 years.
