# Theft charges in Georgia: penalties and your rights

Theft charges in Georgia: how the value of property, breaking into a home and acting as a group affect the penalty, and what to do if accused.
[Property crimes](https://mmlaw.ge/en/property-crime-lawyer/) · October 6, 2026 · 3 min read · Updated: October 7, 2026

Theft is the secret taking of someone else's movable property with the aim of appropriating it — Article 177 of the Criminal Code. The penalty depends mainly on the value and the circumstances.

## Penalties

- Basic case — a fine, house arrest, or 1 to 3 years' imprisonment

- Significant damage (over GEL 150) or breaking into premises — 3 to 5 years

- By a group, repeatedly, breaking into a home, or stealing a vehicle — 4 to 7 years

- A large amount (over GEL 10,000) or by an organised group — 6 to 10 years

## What matters for the defence

- A correct valuation of the property — it decides which part of the article applies

- Whether the taking was secret and aimed at appropriation

- Video footage, identification and seizure records

- Compensating the damage and reaching agreement with the victim

## If you are arrested

Use your right to remain silent and ask for a lawyer. You must be charged within 48 hours of arrest, and within the following 24 hours a court decides on a preventive measure.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the penalty for theft?

Basic case — a fine, house arrest or 1 to 3 years' imprisonment. Damage over GEL 150, breaking into a home, acting as a group or a large amount make it stricter — up to 10 years.

### Why does the value of the property matter?

It decides which part of the article applies: over GEL 150 is significant damage, over GEL 10,000 a large amount. So checking the valuation is an important part of the defence.

### I was arrested for theft. What should I do?

Use your right to remain silent and ask for a lawyer. You must be charged within 48 hours, and within the next 24 hours a court decides on a preventive measure.
