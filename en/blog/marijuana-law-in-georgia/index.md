# Marijuana law in Georgia: what gets you fined?

The rules on marijuana in Georgia: small amounts, using it in public places, minors, and when it becomes a criminal offence.
[Drug offences](https://mmlaw.ge/en/drug-crime-lawyer/) · October 5, 2026 · 3 min read · Updated: October 7, 2026

Many people think marijuana is allowed in Georgia in every case. It isn't — the law punishes many acts with fines or criminal liability.

## Small amounts: a fine

Buying, possessing, transporting or sending a small amount of marijuana is an administrative offence — a GEL 500 fine.

## Where using it is punished

- In any building except a private home — GEL 500–1,000

- On the street, in parks, cafés, public transport and other public places — GEL 800–1,200

- In front of minors or near schools — GEL 1,000–1,500

- Repeat offences — higher fines

## Under 18s

Use by a person under 18 is fined GEL 500–1,000, and more for a repeat offence.

## When it becomes a crime

- A large or especially large amount

- Selling or giving it to someone — even for free

- Bringing it into or out of Georgia

- Growing the plant

The amounts are set in an annex to the law. If you were fined or a case was opened against you, contact a lawyer — fines can be appealed too.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the fine for a small amount of marijuana?

GEL 500 — it is an administrative offence.

### What is the fine for using it in public?

On the street, in parks, cafés, transport and other public places — GEL 800–1,200; in front of minors or near schools — GEL 1,000–1,500.

### When is it a criminal offence?

For a large amount, giving it to someone (even for free), bringing it into or out of Georgia, or growing the plant.
