# Foreigner arrested in Georgia: rights and first steps

Rights of a foreigner arrested in Georgia: a lawyer, a free interpreter, the consulate. What happens in the first 72 hours and the extra risks.
[Criminal defense](https://mmlaw.ge/en/criminal-defense-lawyer/) · October 11, 2026 · 5 min read

When a foreigner is arrested in Georgia, the language, an unfamiliar procedure and distance from family make everything harder. The first hours matter most.

## Your rights

- A lawyer — from the first contact with the investigating authority

- A free interpreter if you don't speak Georgian — in that case a lawyer is also mandatory

- The right to remain silent — you don't have to give a statement

- Having your country's consulate informed — ask for it

- Having a relative informed of your arrest

## What happens after the arrest

You must be charged within 48 hours of the arrest, or released. Within the next 24 hours after the charge, a court decides on a preventive measure — for example, bail or pre-trial detention.

## Why the risk is higher for a foreigner

The prosecution often argues that a foreigner may leave the country, so the risk of a detention request is high. The lawyer's job is to show the court your ties to Georgia — home, job, family, business — and to propose a less severe measure.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Don't sign any document you don't understand

- Don't give explanations without your lawyer — not even in an "informal chat"

- Don't agree to a plea agreement before reviewing the evidence with your lawyer

## Expulsion as a consequence

For a foreigner, a criminal case can have an extra consequence: the court can impose expulsion from Georgia and an entry ban as a penalty. Expulsion can also come up after a sentence has been served. So the defence strategy has to take this risk into account.

## Frequently asked questions

### Am I entitled to a free interpreter?

Yes. If you don't speak Georgian, the state provides an interpreter, and a lawyer is also mandatory.

### Will my consulate be informed?

Yes, if you ask. Ask as soon as you are arrested.

### Can my family hire a lawyer from abroad?

Yes. We sign the engagement remotely and step into the case right away.
