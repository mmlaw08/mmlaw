# Buying an apartment in Georgia: what to check

What to check before buying an apartment in Georgia: the Public Registry extract, the seller, spouse consent, new builds and outstanding debts.
[Real estate](https://mmlaw.ge/en/real-estate-lawyer/) · October 5, 2026 · 4 min read · Updated: October 7, 2026

Buying an apartment is one of the biggest financial decisions. A few checks in advance protect you from big problems.

## 1. Public Registry extract

Check who the owner is, the size of the apartment, and whether there is a mortgage, seizure or prohibition. Get an up-to-date extract.

## 2. The seller

- Check the seller's ID and that they are the owner

- If the apartment was bought during a marriage, the spouse's consent may be needed

- If there are several owners, all of them must agree

- If the seller acts under a power of attorney, check it

## 3. New builds

- Does the developer have a construction permit?

- What stage is construction at?

- In the contract: the completion date and liability for delays

## 4. Debts and payment

Check for utility debts. Pay in a way that is documented — and only after signing the contract or through a secure mechanism.

## 5. Registration

Ownership arises on registration with the Public Registry. Until registration is complete, the apartment isn't legally yours.
