# Arrested for drugs in Georgia: what to do

What to do if you are arrested over drugs in Georgia: your rights, the search, drug testing, deadlines, possible penalties and mistakes to avoid.
[Drug offences](https://mmlaw.ge/en/drug-crime-lawyer/) · October 5, 2026 · 4 min read · Updated: October 7, 2026

Drug arrests happen fast, and a mistake in the first hours can decide the case. Here is what you need to know.

## Your rights when arrested

- The right to remain silent — you don't have to give a statement without a lawyer

- The right to a lawyer from the moment of arrest

- The right to have a family member informed

- The right to read the record and add your comments

## Search and seizure

Watch who is present at the search, what is taken and how it is packed. If the record doesn't match what happened, don't sign it — or add a note.

## Drug testing

Police may ask for a test if they have sufficient grounds. Avoiding a test is itself an administrative offence — a fine of GEL 500–2,000 or administrative detention of up to 60 days.

## Deadlines

You must be charged within 48 hours of arrest, and within the next 24 hours a court decides on a preventive measure. Your lawyer should already be involved by then.

## Possible penalties

- First-time use or possession of a small amount — as a rule, an administrative penalty

- The same again after an administrative penalty — a criminal offence

- Buying and possession — imprisonment of up to 6 years; more for large amounts

- Sale and import — serious crimes with long sentences

## Mistakes to avoid

- "Explaining" without a lawyer

- Signing a record without reading it

- Giving your phone password without legal advice

- Accepting a plea agreement without a lawyer's advice

## Frequently asked questions

### I was arrested over drugs. Do I have to make a statement?

No — you have the right to remain silent and the right to a lawyer from the moment of arrest.

### Can I refuse a drug test?

Avoiding a test is itself an offence — a fine of GEL 500–2,000 or administrative detention of up to 60 days.

### I disagree with the record. What should I do?

Don't sign it, or add a note. Watch who is present at the search, what is taken and how it is packed.
