# Scammed online? How to try to get your money back

What to do if you were scammed online in Georgia: your bank, evidence, reporting to the police — and the "money recovery" scammers.
[Cybercrime](https://mmlaw.ge/en/cybercrime-lawyer/) · October 5, 2026 · 3 min read · Updated: October 7, 2026

With online fraud, time is everything. The faster you act, the better your chances of getting the money back.

## The first hours: what to do

- Contact your bank immediately — block the card and dispute the payment.

- Change your passwords and turn on two-factor authentication.

- Keep everything — messages, links, account numbers, transactions and screenshots.

- Contact a lawyer — they will prepare the complaint to the police.

## Common scams

- Fake online shops and listings

- A "bank employee" calling to ask for codes or card details

- Investment and crypto "opportunities"

- Fake links by SMS or messenger

## Beware of "money recovery" services

After a scam, services often appear promising to recover your money for an upfront fee. As a rule, this is another scam.

## Can the money be recovered?

Sometimes, yes — especially if you contact the bank quickly or the offender is identified. A lawyer helps you prepare the evidence correctly and protects your interests as a victim.

## Frequently asked questions

### I was scammed online. What should I do first?

Contact your bank immediately to block the card and dispute the payment. Then change your passwords and keep all the evidence.

### Can the money be recovered?

Sometimes — especially if you contact the bank quickly or the offender is identified.

### Should I trust a 'money recovery' service?

No. Services that promise to recover your money for an upfront fee are, as a rule, another scam.
