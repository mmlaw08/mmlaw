# How to appeal a fine in Georgia

If you think a fine is unfair: where and how to appeal it in Georgia, what evidence to gather and which mistakes to avoid.
[Administrative law](https://mmlaw.ge/en/administrative-law-lawyer/) · October 5, 2026 · 3 min read · Updated: October 7, 2026

A fine is an administrative decision and can be appealed — if it is groundless or was imposed in breach of the law.

## Step 1: Read the decision carefully

The decision usually states who fined you, on what grounds, where it can be appealed and within what deadline. Note when you received it — the deadline runs from that day.

## Step 2: Gather evidence

- Photos and video recordings

- Witnesses' contact details

- Receipts, tickets, permits — anything that supports your position

## Step 3: File a complaint or lawsuit

Depending on the type of fine, it can be appealed to a superior body or in court. A lawyer will assess which route is faster and more effective.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Missing the deadline — it's short

- Not recording the date you received the decision

- Complaining without evidence

- Ignoring notices

## Frequently asked questions

### Where do I appeal a fine?

Depending on the type — to a superior body or in court. Where and by when is usually stated in the decision itself.

### When does the appeal deadline start?

From the day you received the decision. Note that date — the deadline is short.

### What evidence will I need?

Photos and video, witnesses' contacts, receipts, tickets, permits — anything that supports your position.
