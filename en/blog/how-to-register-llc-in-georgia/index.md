# Registering an LLC in Georgia: what you need

How to register an LLC in Georgia: documents, legal address, charter, timing and what to do after registration — including for foreigners.
[Corporate law](https://mmlaw.ge/en/corporate-lawyer/) · October 5, 2026 · 3 min read · Updated: October 7, 2026

An LLC is the most common business form in Georgia. Registration is fast if the documents are prepared correctly.

## What you need

- A company name

- Details of the partners and the director

- A charter

- A legal address in Georgia — with the owner's consent

## How registration works

The company is registered with the Public Registry, usually within one business day. It is registered as a taxpayer at the same time.

## Can a foreigner do it?

Yes. Foreign individuals and companies can be an LLC's sole partner and director. Depending on the country, documents issued abroad may need an apostille or consular legalisation (documents from some countries need neither) and a certified Georgian translation.

## After registration

- Opening a bank account

- Setting up accounting and understanding your tax obligations

- Signing a shareholder agreement if there are several partners

## Mistakes to avoid

- Using a template charter without adapting it

- Having no written agreement between partners

- Mixing personal and company money

## Frequently asked questions

### What do I need to register an LLC?

A company name, details of the partners and director, a charter, and a legal address in Georgia with the owner's consent.

### How long does registration take?

Usually one business day at the Public Registry. The company is registered as a taxpayer at the same time.

### Can a foreigner set up an LLC?

Yes — foreign individuals and companies can be the sole partner and director.
