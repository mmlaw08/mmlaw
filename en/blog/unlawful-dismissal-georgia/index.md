# Unfairly dismissed in Georgia? What to do

What to do if you were unfairly dismissed in Georgia: asking for written grounds, documents, deadlines and what you can claim in court.
[Labor disputes](https://mmlaw.ge/en/employment-lawyer/) · October 5, 2026 · 4 min read · Updated: October 7, 2026

Losing your job is stressful, but if your employer broke the law you can protect your rights. The key is to act quickly and correctly.

## Step 1: Ask for the written grounds

Ask your employer to explain the grounds for your dismissal in writing. This document matters, because it is exactly what gets examined later.

## Step 2: Keep every document

- Employment contract and amendments

- The dismissal order

- Correspondence with your employer — emails, messages

- Salary statements

## Step 3: Don't miss the deadlines

Deadlines for challenging a dismissal are short, so contact a lawyer as soon as possible — ideally as soon as you receive the written grounds.

## What you can claim

- Reinstatement

- Pay for the period you were forced out of work

- Compensation if reinstatement is impossible or you don't want it

## Mistakes to avoid

- Signing a "voluntary resignation" under pressure

- Deleting documents and correspondence

- Missing the deadline

## Frequently asked questions

### What should I do first?

Ask your employer for the written grounds for dismissal, and keep every document and message.

### What can I claim in court?

Reinstatement, pay for the period you were forced out of work, or compensation if reinstatement is impossible or you don't want it.

### Should I sign a 'voluntary resignation'?

Not under pressure — it is one of the most common mistakes. Deadlines to challenge a dismissal are short, so contact a lawyer soon.
