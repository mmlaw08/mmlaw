# Accepting an inheritance in Georgia from abroad

How to accept an inheritance in Georgia while living abroad: the 6-month deadline, power of attorney, documents and what happens if you missed the deadline.
[Inheritance](https://mmlaw.ge/en/inheritance-lawyer/) · October 5, 2026 · 4 min read · Updated: October 7, 2026

If you live abroad and a family member in Georgia has died, you can accept the inheritance without coming — through a power of attorney.

## The key point: the 6-month deadline

An inheritance must be accepted within 6 months of its opening — as a rule, the date of death. The application to the notary can be filed by your representative.

## How to issue a power of attorney

- At a Georgian embassy or consulate — no further certification needed

- With a local notary — depending on the country, an apostille or legalisation may be needed, plus a certified Georgian translation

- Online with a notary in Georgia — if you have a Georgian passport: you join by video call, with two witnesses present at the notary

Prepare the text with your lawyer in advance so it covers all the powers needed.

## Documents you'll need

- Death certificate

- Proof of kinship

- Your ID document

- Property documents or a Public Registry extract

## If you missed the deadline

If you missed it for a valid reason, a court can extend it. Contact a lawyer as soon as possible.

## What happens next

The notary issues a certificate of inheritance, and the property is registered in your name with the Public Registry. You can then sell or rent it out — also by power of attorney.

## Frequently asked questions

### Within what time must I accept an inheritance?

Within 6 months of its opening — as a rule, the date of death.

### Do I need to come to Georgia?

No — you can act by power of attorney: at an embassy or consulate, with a local notary, or, if you have a Georgian passport, online with a notary in Georgia.

### What if I missed the 6-month deadline?

If you missed it for a valid reason, a court can extend it. Contact a lawyer as soon as possible.
