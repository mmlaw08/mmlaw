# Drunk driving in Georgia: fines, licence, prison

Drunk driving rules in Georgia: alcohol limits, licence suspension, refusing a test, and when it becomes a criminal offence.
[Criminal defense](https://mmlaw.ge/en/criminal-defense-lawyer/) · October 6, 2026 · 3 min read · Updated: October 7, 2026

Driving under the influence of alcohol is, as a rule, an administrative offence (Article 116 of the Code of Administrative Offences). It becomes a crime when someone is hurt, or when a person drives under the influence of drugs.

## Licence suspension

- 0.3 to 0.7 per mille — 6 months

- Over 0.7 per mille — 1 year

- Refusing a test — the same as over 0.7: 1 year

## When it gets more serious

- Drunk driving during a suspension — GEL 2,000, administrative detention of 5–15 days and the suspension extended by 1 year

- Drunk driving that causes minor damage to a vehicle, property or health — GEL 2,000 and a 3-year suspension

- Repeat offences or driving without a licence — fines up to GEL 3,000 and administrative detention

## When it is a crime

- Driving under the influence of drugs is a criminal offence (Article 276)

- A drunk driver causes less serious harm to someone's health — 2 to 5 years

- Serious harm — 4 to 6 years

- A death — 6 to 9 years

Much in these cases depends on how the test was done. A lawyer checks whether the test and the record were correct. Fines and suspensions can be appealed.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long will my licence be suspended?

0.3 to 0.7 per mille — 6 months; over 0.7 per mille — 1 year.

### Can I refuse a breath test?

Refusing is punished the same as over 0.7 per mille — a 1-year licence suspension.

### When does drunk driving become a crime?

When the drunk driver injures someone or causes a death. Driving under the influence of drugs is a criminal offence in itself.
