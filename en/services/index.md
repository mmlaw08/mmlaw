Property crimes are covered by Chapter XXIV of the Criminal Code. The penalty depends mainly on the amount: over GEL 150 counts as significant damage, over GEL 10,000 as a large amount.

Economic and financial cases

## [Fraud cases](https://mmlaw.ge/en/fraud-lawyer/)

Defence against fraud charges and recovering money for victims — investment, loan, pyramid and crypto fraud.
Fraud is taking someone else's property or obtaining a property right by deception, in order to keep it (Article 180 of the Criminal Code). The key question is whether there was intent to deceive from the start — if an obligation simply wasn't met, it is often a civil dispute.
[Fraud lawyer](https://mmlaw.ge/en/fraud-lawyer/)

## [Financial crime victims](https://mmlaw.ge/en/financial-crime-victims/)

Under the 2025 rules: compensation, freezing property and a ban on the convicted person leaving Georgia.
In September 2025 a victim compensation procedure was added to the Criminal Procedure Code (Articles 165³–165⁸). A guilty verdict orders the convicted person to pay compensation if they are convicted of, for example, large-scale fraud, extortion or embezzlement, property damage over GEL 10,000, abuse of authority in a company or computer crime — and the victim has not waived compensation in writing.
[Victims of financial crime](https://mmlaw.ge/en/financial-crime-victims/)

## [Tax disputes](https://mmlaw.ge/en/tax-lawyer/)

Tax audits, appealing tax demands, reducing tax debt and defence against tax evasion charges (Article 218).
A tax dispute has two stages: the complaint goes first to the Revenue Service, then to the Dispute Resolution Council at the Ministry of Finance or to court. The deadline to appeal is 30 days from receiving the decision — missing it almost rules out a dispute.
[Tax lawyer](https://mmlaw.ge/en/tax-lawyer/)

## [Money laundering cases](https://mmlaw.ge/en/money-laundering-lawyer/)

Defence against Article 194 charges, frozen bank accounts and proving the lawful origin of assets.
Money laundering (legalising illegal income) is giving illegal or unexplained property a lawful appearance in order to hide its origin (Article 194). Unexplained property is property for which there are no documents showing it was lawfully obtained — so documents and financial history decide these cases.
[Money laundering defence lawyer](https://mmlaw.ge/en/money-laundering-lawyer/)

## [Asset freezing and confiscation](https://mmlaw.ge/en/asset-freezing-and-confiscation/)

Freezing of property and accounts in criminal cases, confiscation and unexplained-wealth cases.
In a criminal case a court can freeze the property of the accused or a connected person, including bank accounts, if there is information that the property may be hidden or spent, or was obtained through crime (Article 151 of the Criminal Procedure Code). A verdict can confiscate the object and instrument of the crime and property obtained through crime (Article 52 of the Criminal Code).
[Asset freezing and confiscation lawyer](https://mmlaw.ge/en/asset-freezing-and-confiscation/)

## [Abuse-of-office cases](https://mmlaw.ge/en/abuse-of-office-lawyer/)

Defending officials and company managers in abuse-of-authority and exceeding-authority cases.
Abuse of official authority (Article 332) and exceeding authority (Article 333) by an official are crimes if they cause a substantial breach of a person's, society's or the state's lawful interests. For private company managers, Article 220 — abuse of authority in a company — and Article 220¹, negligence, apply.
[Abuse of office and exceeding authority](https://mmlaw.ge/en/abuse-of-office-lawyer/)

## [Smuggling and customs cases](https://mmlaw.ge/en/smuggling-and-customs-lawyer/)

Defence under Article 214: moving goods past customs control, false declarations, jewellery and valuables.
Moving a large amount of goods across the customs border past customs control, secretly, with a false document or with false data in the customs declaration is a crime (Article 214). A large amount means a customs value over GEL 15,000, an especially large amount over GEL 25,000; for goods hidden in a cache or brought by crossing the border illegally, the thresholds are GEL 5,000 and GEL 15,000.
[Smuggling and customs offences](https://mmlaw.ge/en/smuggling-and-customs-lawyer/)

## [Director liability and insolvency](https://mmlaw.ge/en/director-liability-and-insolvency/)

Liability of LLC directors and partners, insolvency and creditors' claims.
A director must manage the company's affairs in good faith and with reasonable care. If they breach this duty, they can be liable to the company for the damage, and in serious cases also criminally — for example, for abuse of authority in a company (Article 220), tax evasion or embezzlement.
[Director liability and company insolvency](https://mmlaw.ge/en/director-liability-and-insolvency/)

## [Drug possession cases](https://mmlaw.ge/en/drug-possession-lawyer/)

Defence against drug purchase and possession charges: Article 260, quantity thresholds, small amounts and administrative cases.
Unlawfully buying or keeping a narcotic drug is punished under Article 260 of the Criminal Code of Georgia — if the amount exceeds the small amount. Buying, keeping or using a small amount is usually an administrative offence; repeating it becomes a crime under Article 273.
[Drug possession lawyer](https://mmlaw.ge/en/drug-possession-lawyer/)

## [Removing a criminal record](https://mmlaw.ge/en/criminal-record-removal/)

When a criminal record expires automatically and how to have it removed early through the court.
Under Article 79 of the Criminal Code, a person has a criminal record from the day the guilty verdict enters into force until the record expires or is removed. An expired or removed record is no longer taken into account.
[Removing a criminal record](https://mmlaw.ge/en/criminal-record-removal/)

## [Early release on parole](https://mmlaw.ge/en/early-release-on-parole/)

When a convicted person can be released early, who decides, and how to prepare for the board.
Under Article 72 of the Criminal Code, early release from a fixed-term prison sentence is decided by the local board of the Special Penitentiary Service; for community service, corrective labour or house arrest — by a court. The rule does not apply to prisoners held in a special-risk facility.
[Early release on parole](https://mmlaw.ge/en/early-release-on-parole/)

## [Pyramid scheme and investment fraud cases](https://mmlaw.ge/en/pyramid-schemes-investment-fraud/)

Crypto, forex and 'high-return' schemes: recovering money for victims and defending those accused of organising or taking part.
These schemes are usually charged as fraud (Article 180), and the money flows can also amount to money laundering (Article 194). Since 2025, a person convicted of large-scale fraud is ordered to pay the victim compensation and cannot leave Georgia until they pay.
[Pyramid schemes and investment fraud](https://mmlaw.ge/en/pyramid-schemes-investment-fraud/)

## [Help with blackmail over private photos](https://mmlaw.ge/en/blackmail-private-photos/)

Threatened with the release of private photos or information? We act for the victim: the case, content removal, compensation.
Several crimes can be involved at once: extortion (Article 181) if money or property is demanded; violation of the secret of private life (Article 157¹); spreading personal data (Article 157); secretly recording a private conversation (Article 158).
[Blackmail with private photos](https://mmlaw.ge/en/blackmail-private-photos/)

## [Time-barred loan cases](https://mmlaw.ge/en/time-barred-loans/)

When a loan becomes time-barred, what restarts the period and how to defend yourself in court or with a collector.
Under Article 129 of the Civil Code, the limitation period for contractual claims — including loans — is 3 years, and 6 years for contracts concerning real estate. For periodic (e.g. monthly) payments it is 3 years, counted separately for each payment. The period starts when the creditor learned or should have learned of the breach — for a loan, usually from the missed payment date.
[Time-barred loans in Georgia: limitation period and defence](https://mmlaw.ge/en/time-barred-loans/)

## [Restraining and protective order cases](https://mmlaw.ge/en/restraining-protective-orders/)

Requesting, appealing and cancelling orders, and breach cases — for victims and for the person the order was issued against.
A restraining order is issued by the police for up to 1 month and takes effect immediately. A protective order is issued by a court for up to 9 months; if the threat continues, it can be extended by no more than 3 months. An order can bar the abuser from the shared home, from approaching the victim, or from using weapons, or impose electronic monitoring.
[Restraining and protective orders](https://mmlaw.ge/en/restraining-protective-orders/)

## [Removing a seizure on accounts and property](https://mmlaw.ge/en/removing-seizure-on-account/)

How to lift an enforcement seizure on a bank account, salary or property — complaints, settlement and time limits.
A seizure is usually imposed in enforcement proceedings — to enforce a court decision, a notary's writ or a fine. How to lift it depends on its basis and how long ago enforcement started.

## [Pardon and amnesty](https://mmlaw.ge/en/pardon-and-amnesty/)

The difference between a pardon and an amnesty, who decides, and how to prepare a pardon request.
A pardon is an individual act: under the Constitution, convicted persons are pardoned by the President of Georgia. Requests are first reviewed by the Pardon Commission, and the President makes the final decision. The criteria and procedure are set by the President and change from time to time.
[Presidential pardon and amnesty](https://mmlaw.ge/en/pardon-and-amnesty/)

## [Driving licence suspension cases](https://mmlaw.ge/en/driving-licence-suspension/)

When a driving licence is suspended — drink-driving, refusing a test, unpaid fines — and how to appeal.
Under Article 116 of the Code of Administrative Offences, drink-driving at 0.3–0.7‰ leads to a 6-month suspension, and over 0.7‰ or refusing the test to a 1-year suspension. If it also causes minor damage to a vehicle, property or health — a GEL 2,000 fine and a 3-year suspension.
[Driving licence suspension in Georgia: 6 months, 1, 3 years](https://mmlaw.ge/en/driving-licence-suspension/)

## [Appealing fines](https://mmlaw.ge/en/appealing-fines/)

Appealing patrol, camera and other administrative fines within 10 days — to the higher authority and in court.
Under the Code of Administrative Offences, a decision imposing a fine can be appealed within 10 days of being received. A deadline missed for a valid reason can be restored. A decision by an authority or official is usually appealed first to the higher authority, and its decision then to court.

## [Compensation for moral damage](https://mmlaw.ge/en/moral-damage-compensation/)

When you can claim compensation for moral (non-pecuniary) damage — injury, harm to reputation, unlawful detention.
Under Article 413 of the Civil Code, money for non-pecuniary (moral) damage can be claimed only in cases set by law — in a reasonable and fair amount. For bodily injury or harm to health, moral damage can always be claimed.
[Compensation for moral damage](https://mmlaw.ge/en/moral-damage-compensation/)

## [Establishing paternity](https://mmlaw.ge/en/establishing-paternity/)

Establishing and challenging paternity: DNA testing, deadlines and the consequences — child support, inheritance, surname.
Under Article 1190 of the Civil Code, the parentage of a child of unmarried parents is established by the parents' joint declaration. Without one, paternity is established in court — on the application of a parent, the child's guardian, the person supporting the child, or the child after reaching adulthood.

## [Taking a child abroad — consent and disputes](https://mmlaw.ge/en/taking-a-child-abroad/)

Whether the other parent's consent is needed to take a child abroad, the passport, and what happens when parents disagree.
Under Article 8 of the Law on the Rules for Georgian Citizens Leaving and Entering Georgia, a child under 16 can travel abroad temporarily with the permission of one legal representative (parent) and accompanied by an adult with legal capacity. From 16 to 18, if not married, with the consent of one legal representative.

## [Challenging a dismissal](https://mmlaw.ge/en/unfair-dismissal/)

Dismissed? Find out if it was lawful: asking for written reasons within 30 days, the lawsuit, reinstatement and lost pay.
Under Article 47 of the Labour Code, an employment contract can be ended only on grounds listed in the law — for example, redundancy, a serious breach of duties, mutual agreement or the end of a fixed term. Dismissal on discriminatory grounds is prohibited, as is dismissal from notifying the employer of pregnancy until the end of maternity and childcare leave — with exceptions set by law.
[Unfair dismissal](https://mmlaw.ge/en/unfair-dismissal/)

## [Protecting employee rights — leave, maternity, salary](https://mmlaw.ge/en/labour-code-leave-salary/)

The Labour Code in plain words: working hours, overtime, leave, maternity leave, late salary and the final settlement.
Normal working time must not exceed 40 hours a week (48 in certain continuous-operation workplaces), and rest between working days must be at least 12 hours. Overtime is worked by agreement and paid at an increased hourly rate — or, by agreement, with extra time off.
[Labour Code of Georgia: leave, maternity, salary](https://mmlaw.ge/en/labour-code-leave-salary/)

## [Removal from the debtors' register](https://mmlaw.ge/en/debtors-register/)

What being on the debtors' register means, how to check it, what is restricted, and how to get removed.
Under Article 19¹ of the Law on Enforcement Proceedings, a person is entered on the debtors' register as soon as enforcement of a money claim starts against them. The register is kept by the National Bureau of Enforcement; its data are public, and an extract is issued no later than the next working day after the application, for a fee. A debtor whose debt is secured by a mortgage or pledge, for example, is not entered.

What counts as joint property, how a flat, loans and a business are divided, property in one spouse's name, and the 3-year deadline.
Under Article 1158 of the Civil Code, property acquired during marriage is the spouses' joint property unless a marriage contract says otherwise — even if one spouse looked after the home and children and had no income of their own. On division the shares are equal unless agreed otherwise.

## [Making and challenging a will](https://mmlaw.ge/en/wills-in-georgia/)

How to make a valid will, which form to choose, who is entitled to a compulsory share and how to challenge a will within 2 years.
A will can be made by an adult with legal capacity who, at that moment, can reasonably judge their own actions. A will must be made in person — it cannot be made through a representative. Only spouses can make a joint will, about inheriting from each other. You can change or revoke a will at any time.

Someone won't pay you back? Demand letter, lawsuit, freezing assets, enforcement and a notary's writ of execution — even without court.

The list of dependent persons is kept by the Revenue Service. A person can be added in two ways: by their own application to the Revenue Service, or by a judge's order issued on a family member's application. A spouse or child ran up gambling debts? Who owes the debt, whether the family flat can be taken and how to protect your share.
Under Article 1170 of the Civil Code, one spouse's debt is collected from their personal property and their share of joint property. Joint marital property can be used only if a court finds that the borrowed money served the common interests of the whole family — and money lost gambling usually doesn't.

Girao — paying a lump sum to live in a flat rent-free: notarised contract, registry registration, checks and what if the owner won't repay.
Under Article 286 of the Civil Code, a private lender that isn't a supervised financial institution generally may not take real estate as security for a loan to an individual. Mortgage contracts in Georgia: notary and registration, ranking of several mortgages, private-lender limits, the interest cap and enforcement disputes.
A mortgage exists only once registered in the Public Registry. A mortgage securing a private loan must be notarised — this doesn't apply to banks, microbanks, microfinance organisations or credit unions (Civil Code, Article 289). With several mortgages, the one registered first has priority.

Illegally obtaining or keeping Lyrica (pregabalin), tramadol and other psychotropic or potent medicines: amounts and penalties under Article 261.
