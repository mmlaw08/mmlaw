## [Drug offences](https://mmlaw.ge/en/drug-crime-lawyer/)

Defence in cases of buying, possessing, selling and importing drugs — and against fines and administrative detention.
Drug offences are covered by Chapter XXXIII of the Criminal Code of Georgia. The penalty depends mainly on the act (buying and possession, making and transporting, sale, import) and on the amount — small, large or especially large. The amounts are set in an annex to the law.

## [Cybercrime](https://mmlaw.ge/en/cybercrime-lawyer/)

Defense in cybercrime cases and help for victims of online fraud, hacked accounts and blackmail.
Cybercrime covers unauthorised access to computer systems, unlawful use or damage of data, and the use of tools designed for such acts. Traditional crimes — fraud, extortion, threats and invasion of privacy — are also increasingly committed online.
[Cybercrime lawyer](https://mmlaw.ge/en/cybercrime-lawyer/)

## [Property crimes](https://mmlaw.ge/en/property-crime-lawyer/)

Defence in theft, robbery, fraud, extortion, embezzlement and misappropriation cases.
Property crimes are covered by Chapter XXIV of the Criminal Code. The penalty depends mainly on the amount: over GEL 150 counts as significant damage, over GEL 10,000 as a large amount.
[Property crime lawyer](https://mmlaw.ge/en/property-crime-lawyer/)

## [Corruption and official crimes](https://mmlaw.ge/en/corruption-crime-lawyer/)

Defence in bribery, trading in influence and abuse of official power cases.
Corruption and official crimes are in Chapter XXXIX of the Criminal Code. These are serious charges: taking a bribe carries at least 6 years and often a ban from holding office.
