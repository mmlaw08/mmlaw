Privacy policy

How MMLAW processes and protects your personal data: what we collect, why we use it and what rights you have.

What we collect

Only what you choose to share with us: your name, phone, email and a description of your matter — when you call, write or use the form. Form messages are delivered to our inbox through the FormSubmit email service. Nothing you type into the forms is sent to analytics services.

Analytics and cookies

Only with your consent, we use Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC) to see which pages are useful and to improve the website. It collects general data: pages visited, device and browser type, approximate country and city, and how you reached the site. Google Analytics does not store IP addresses. It uses the cookies _ga and _ga_*, kept for up to 2 years. Advertising features and Google Signals are switched off. Data may be processed outside Georgia, including in the USA. Google's script is not loaded on the website until you consent.

Your choice

Consent is voluntary. You can change or withdraw it at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of the website; withdrawing it deletes the analytics cookies. Your browser also remembers your choice and the light/dark theme — this is needed for the website to work and is not shared with anyone.

How we use it

Only to contact you and to provide legal services. We never sell your data or share it with third parties, except where required by law.

Confidentiality

Everything you share with us about your matter is protected by attorney–client privilege.

Your rights

Under the Law of Georgia on Personal Data Protection you may request access to, correction or deletion of your data. Write to info@mmlaw.ge.
